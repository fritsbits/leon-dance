# Cross-sector comparator — Debateville

Not an artistic peer (those are in [Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md): Ultima Vez, kabinet k,
les ballets C de la B, fABULEUS). Debateville is a **funding-ecology + site-strategy
comparator**: a different sector with a near-identical money structure and audience logic to
Leon, whose website is explicitly built as an impact/traction instrument. Used to make
"peer credibility level" ([Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md) → legitimacy job) *concrete*. Facts
sourced; comparative judgements ⚠️.

## Organisation snapshot (sourced)

Debateville is a Brussels **vzw / nonprofit**, founded **2020**, running a debate &
public-speaking programme for **urban youth aged 10–14** (afterschool classes, school
debatelabs, summer programmes). Founder/director **Sophie Buysse**. HQ Jozef-II-straat 20,
Brussels (origin at BeCentral). Bilingual **NL/FR**. Homepage traction band:
**480 young people weekly · 9 regions · 200 mentors · 90% attendance · 80% report
significant learning gains**; LinkedIn states **>3,000 young people reached annually**, team
"2–10 / 42 total". Sources: [debateville.org](https://debateville.org/),
[LinkedIn](https://be.linkedin.com/company/debateville),
[Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/debateville). ⚠️ Figures vary by page (the
About page still shows an older "210 teenagers / 6 regions / 54 mentors" snapshot) — the
homepage band is the load-bearing public number; even a polished org carries stale figures
in corners.

⚠️ **Significance.** Debateville is **independent, cross-sector corroboration of the core

also reaching its beneficiary offline, has built its site as a legitimacy/funder/partner/
recruit instrument — *and*, unlike the four dance peers, it also runs the explicit
funder/press/impact **portal** those peers don't. That sharpens the honest refinement in
[website-purpose](11-website-purpose.md) → peer triangulation: such a portal is a proven
move for a project-funded org seeking durability, not just speculative differentiation.

## How the Debateville site works as an impact/traction machine

Seven patterns, observed live 2026-05-19. The site is architected to *prove the org is real,
effective and fundable*:

1. **Homepage metric band.** Five traction numbers above the fold (see snapshot). Instant
   "this is real and at scale" signal. → Leon never quantifies itself anywhere.
2. **Impact page = a 3-tier evidence ladder.** (1) *external academic* ("Research approves"
   — Univ. of Vermont *Debating the evidence*; CASEL meta-analysis cited on About);
   (2) *internal survey %* (2020–21: 82% would recommend, 86% learned significantly, 85%
   persuade better, 80% respond respectfully); (3) *output + mentor 0–10 ratings* (140
   intro workshops, 43 summer sessions / 1,318 youth). A credibility *architecture*, not a
   brag. Source: [Impact](https://debateville.org/over-ons/impact/).
3. **Audience-first IA — "Voor wie…" (For whom…).** Primary nav segmented by *who you are*
   — Youth & parents / Schools / Donors & partners / Mentors — each with its own page and
   its own ask. Source: [debateville.org](https://debateville.org/).
4. **Annual impact-report hub.** Five years of reports (2020–2025), flip-book + PDF
   download. Durability/accountability signal for funders. Source:
   [Jaarrapport](https://debateville.org/jaarrapport/).
5. **Tiered, captioned partner/funder wall.** Grouped *Financial · Pro-bono · Infrastructure*,
   each named. Reads as an endorsement network, not footer decoration. Source:
   [Donors & partners](https://debateville.org/voor-wie/donors-en-partners/).
6. **Press + Awards as their own nav items** ("Pers", "In de prijzen") — third-party
   validation surfaced, not buried.
7. **Owned recruitment funnels.** Mentor sign-up + atelier registration with scarcity copy
   ("Schrijf je in… week van 28 september 2026"). The *only* place the site is a funnel is
   for **volunteers/mentors and co-creators — not core beneficiaries**. Independently
   matches the peer pattern in [website-purpose](11-website-purpose.md) → triangulation
   (participation = showcase; volunteer/co-creator recruitment = an owned funnel).

⚠️ **Flag, not recommend:** Debateville also runs an **Open Collective** radical-transparency
micro-donation rail + tax-deductible individual giving. Total raised there is small
(~€5.6k lifetime) — the real money is the foundation/public portfolio; Open Collective is a
*trust signal*, not the funding base. Whether Leon has or wants an individual-donor audience
is a client question, not a scope recommendation.

## Scope ideas for the Leon site (⚠️ proposed — each maps to a cited finding)

1. **Proof / Impact page** with the 3-tier ladder, **translated to Leon**: academic method
   (the *Inviter/Mixing/Reversing/Affirming* method, Profs Janssens & Steyaert — surfaced,
   not buried on one EN page) → qualitative evidence (testimonials, the 58-photo/film
   archive, partner quotes) → honest output metrics Leon *can* own (years active, # schools,
   ateliers/year from the agenda data, # co-creators, public-show reach). Maps to
   [website-purpose](11-website-purpose.md) "credibility/legitimacy" + "proof/archive";
   [Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md) legitimacy job.
2. **Audience-segmented IA** — top-level routes by *who you are*: Co-creators · Schools ·
   Programmers & venues · Funders & partners · (Press). Directly fixes the "strong content,
   weak IA — serves everyone equally" problem ([Desk Research](10-desk-research.md) #5) and
   the explicit-primary-audience recommendation ([website-purpose](11-website-purpose.md)).
3. **Annual impact-report hub** — even one inaugural report is a step-change in funder
   legitimacy; lowest-tech, highest-ROI legitimacy move (build the shell, fill yearly).
4. **Tiered + captioned funder/partner wall** — restructure the existing ~35-logo flat
   footer ([Site Audit](20-site-audit.md)) into Funders / Co-producers / Venues / In-kind,
   one line each. Reuses an asset Leon already maintains.
5. **Press & recognition page** — aggregate existing coverage (BRUZZ etc.) + the
   Ultima Vez/*Birds* lineage + *Kosmos* 2027 (KANAL/KVS co-production) as forward traction.
6. **Owned co-creator recruitment funnel** for *Mariage* — retire the external MS Forms
   ([Site Audit](20-site-audit.md) problem #3); the one peer-confirmed legitimate
   "site as funnel".

## ⚠️ Steer: lead qualitatively, not with a metrics dashboard

Debateville can run a %-survey dashboard because its outcomes are skills/education
(test scores, recommend-rate). Leon's value is **relational/artistic** — copying the
quantified model would misrepresent the practice and is not defensible from the evidence
Leon holds. Adopt Debateville's *structure* (claim → external validation → evidence →
testimonial → report), lead it with **qualitative/artistic proof**, and use only output
counts Leon can honestly stand behind. (Confirmed direction with the user 2026-05-19.)
