## Mission / vision / values / method

The clearest articulation is `leon.dance/missie-visie-eng`:

- **Mission:** "Through dance and music, Leon invites people of all ages, genders, bodies, and
  backgrounds to come together in an open, playful, and respectful community."
- **Vision:** a safe meeting space against urban loneliness/polarisation; non-verbal connection
  (dance, music, touch, eye contact, shared meals); dismantling hierarchies and reversing
  stereotypical roles.
- **Six values:** Horizontal · Free of Judgment · Welcoming · Vulnerable · Experimental ·
  Playful.
- **Method** — grounded in academic research by Profs **Maddy Janssens & Chris Steyaert**
  (organisation/diversity scholars, KU Leuven ⚠️ affiliation inferred): four practices —
  **Inviter, Mixing, Reversing, Affirming**.

⚠️ This is a genuinely distinctive, research-backed positioning — a strong asset for the new
site, currently buried in a single manual EN page.

## Funding model

Sources: **Vlaams Subsidieregister** export →
`docs/raw/research/subsidieregister-vlaanderen-leon-2026-05-18.md`;
**browser desk-research dossier** (VGC + CJM PDFs retrieved in full) →
`docs/raw/research/leon-vzw-desk-research-browser-2026-05-18.md`.

- Site states: Leon vzw is "supported by the **Flemish government and the Flemish Community
  Commission (VGC)**." Source: `leon.dance/contact`.
- **At a glance (confirmed, registries, 2026-05-18):** funding is **project/short-cycle only** —
  **Decreet Bovenlokale Cultuurwerking (2018)** €84,250 (2023), **Polsslag Brussel** €88,000
  (2023)/€78,000 (2025), and **VGC meerjarige werkingssubsidie €14,840/yr (2023–2025)**.
  **No Kunstendecreet** (no structural *werkingssubsidie*, none in projectsubsidies 2026 r1) —
  ≪ structurally-funded peers (Ultima Vez €1.04M/yr). Full breakdown
  [see below](#funding-model).
- ⚠️ Seppe Baeyens' major participatory works — *Tornar* (2015), *INVITED* (2018), *Birds*
  (2021) — were created **under Ultima Vez** (Wim Vandekeybus' structurally-funded company),
  not under Leon vzw. Source: [kvs.be](https://www.kvs.be/en/maker/seppe-baeyens),
  [ultimavez.com/fr/productions/birds](https://ultimavez.com/fr/productions/birds). Leon vzw
  appears to be the newer vehicle for his own participatory practice (Atelier Leon, Mariage,
  Leon à l'école). Important for understanding scale, funding maturity, and how they work.
- ⚠️ Venues now credit *Birds* to **"Leon"** (La Monnaie/De Munt, CAMPO) — repertoire/credit
  migrating Ultima Vez → Leon. Full analysis [see below](#lineage-and-production-model).

Three confirmed public instruments, **all project/short-cycle — no structural arts funding**:

- **[REG] Decreet Bovenlokale Cultuurwerking (2018)** projectsubsidie, via Departement CJM:
  **€84,250 awarded (2023)**; paid €75,825 (2023) + €8,425 (2024) = 90% advance + 10% balance.
- **[REG] Polsslag Brussel** ("Subsidie voor projecten in Brussel"), via Agentschap
  Binnenlands Bestuur: **€88,000 awarded (2023)** (€44,000 paid 2023) and **€78,000 awarded
  (2025)** (€80,000 paid 2025); +€26,400 paid 2024. ⚠️ Award vs. paid timing differs by year.
- **[REG] VGC meerjarige werkingssubsidie kunsten 2023–2025 = €14,840/year** (VGC PDF
  retrieved in full). Small. For scale, Ultima Vez gets €10,600/yr in the *same* VGC list —
  i.e. VGC amounts are minor top-ups, not a structural base. This **confirms the site's
  "supported by … VGC" claim** and closes that [GAP].
- **[REG] verified-negative — no Kunstendecreet at all:** absent from the full Kunstendecreet
  **werkingssubsidies 2023–2027** table (>160 beneficiaries scanned) and from **projectsubsidies
  2026 ronde 1** (full list); not surfaced in 2022–2025 project rounds (⚠️ those PDFs not
  opened line-by-line — strong, not absolute). Also absent from VGC éénjarige/project/traject
  2025.

- **⚠️ Interpretation (reinforced):** Leon is institutionally framed as **cultural-participation
  / Brussels-cohesion work**, *not* a structurally-funded *kunsten* company. Total public
  funding is **low six figures, project-by-project** (≈€187k Flemish project awards in 2023 +
  €14,840 VGC/yr; €78k 2025) versus structurally-funded peers in the €1M+/yr range (Ultima Vez
  €1,040,387/yr Kunstendecreet — see [Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md)). Funding is **lumpy and
  insecure**, consistent with the 2021 founding → 2023 board → Jan-2025 payroll trajectory.
  This sharpens the website's **legitimacy job**: credible *both* in the arts field and to
  supra-local / Brussels-policy funders.
- **[CLIENT 2026-05-19] Kunstendecreet ambition — confirmed.** Leon **is bidding for
  Kunstendecreet *structural* funding for 2028–2032**: dossier deadline **1 December 2026**,
  jury assessment **≈ January–July 2027**. The website is explicitly Leon's *visitekaartje*
  for that jury → the legitimacy job is now **dated and load-bearing**, not inferred. The
  *participatie* function is the one Leon fills under the decree. Source:
  [Client Kickoff §1](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md#1-the-strategic-driver-kunstendecreet-20282032).

## Lineage and production model

- Seppe Baeyens' practice was **incubated inside Ultima Vez**: *Atelier Quartier* (launched
  Jan 2017 — "no registration, no commitment, free, door literally open") is the direct
  template for **Atelier Leon**. *Tornar* (2015), *INVITED* (2018), *Birds* (2021) were
  **Ultima Vez** productions. **[SRC]** Demos; kvs.be; ultimavez.com.
- **Leon vzw (founded 2021) is the independent vehicle**; it started operating beyond Ultima
  Vez ~2022 (joined the Circuit/Antonin Artaud network in 2022). Repertoire split is now clear:
  - ***Birds* (2021):** production stays **Ultima Vez**; **Leon takes tour direction from
    2023** (La Monnaie: "Production: Ultima Vez — Tour direction: Leon"). Coproducers KVS,
    STUK (Creative Europe *DANCE ON PASS ON DREAM ON*), tanzhaus nrw, Festival de Marseille.
  - ***Mariage* (2024–):** **Leon's own production**, premiere **21.09.2024, MolenFest 2024**
    (Molenbeek/Brussels 2030 & KANAL); recurring free editions through 2026.
  - ***Kosmos* (2027):** Leon is a **co-producer with KANAL-Centre Pompidou & KVS** (KVS
    08–18.04.2027) — a forward pipeline beyond Ultima-Vez-grown work.
  **[SRC]** browser dossier §9; lamonnaiedemunt.be; kvs.be. (Earlier [GAP] resolved.)
- **Nomadic, no own venue.** Working base "Lion City", Ossegemstraat 53, Molenbeek; ateliers
  hosted at Maison des Cultures (Molenbeek) and GC Pianofabriek (St-Gilles) — host venues, not
  owned. Works are produced/presented via partner houses & festivals (KVS, KANAL, De Munt,
  BRONKS, Cultureghem, CAMPO). **⚠️** No evidence of a dedicated touring/distribution office.
- **⚠️ Not yet institutionally embedded:** no Leon profile in the **Kunstenpunt databank**
  (only Ultima Vez appears); not in the **Kunstendecreet 2023–27 werkingssubsidies** list.
  Consistent with a young (2021) structure carrying Ultima-Vez-grown repertoire.

## How they work

- **Nomadic, participation-first.** "A Brussels-based and nomadic workspace with dance as its
  binding element"; the *processes* preceding productions matter as much as the productions
  ("the power of meeting and connection"). Public space over the theatre. Source: `leon.dance`,
  `leon.dance/who-we-are-1`.
- **Recurring rhythm:** weekly *Atelier Leon* (Sat Molenbeek / Wed St-Gilles); monthly *Leon
  rond de tafel* coordination+peer-learning gatherings at Lion City; project cycles like
  *Mariage* touring partner venues/festivals; *Leon à l'école* in schools. Source:
  `leon.dance/what-we-do`.
- **Embedded in a dense partner network** (~35 footer logos): community/welfare (D'Broej,
  Zonnelied, BUSO Cardijnschool, Maison des Cultures), arts houses (KVS, KANAL, BOZAR, De
  Munt, Ultima Vez, P.A.R.T.S.), academia (KU Leuven, VUB), city/policy (VGC, Brussels2030,
  Demos, Lasso). Source: home footer. ⚠️ Footer logos conflate funders, presenters, and
  community partners — the actual relationship type per partner is unknown.
- **Working culture (⚠️ lineage context, Ultima-Vez-era):** The Demos field piece on the
  Baeyens/De Wit Atelier Quartier practice describes a **deliberative, "searching" culture**
  ("the team don't always agree"), **partners-as-equals** but with practical asymmetries, and
  a candid admission of resourcing limits at "policy level, budget and team management, future
  vision". **[SRC]**
  [demos.be](https://demos.be/kenniscentrum/artikel/de-deur-staat-letterlijk-open-op-bezoek-bij-ultima-vez).
  ⚠️ This predates Leon vzw and describes Ultima Vez; carried here as lineage/DNA, not a current
  Leon statement — but the precarity/professionalisation tension is plausibly still live.
