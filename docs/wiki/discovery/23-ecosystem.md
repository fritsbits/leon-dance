# Ecosystem — comparable collectives & how peers handle web/funding

Not "competitors" — peers in participatory / socially-engaged dance in Brussels & Flanders.
Used to benchmark how a Leon-like organisation handles website and funding. Facts sourced;
comparative judgements ⚠️.

## Reference framing

The Flemish/Brussels dance field has an explicit push to "break open the canon… let in more
social, cultural and gender diversity." Children-and-adults-on-stage and participatory practice
is an established sub-field (kabinet k, Nat Gras, Tuning People, fABULEUS). The
**Flanders Arts Institute / Kunstenpunt** maintains sector dossiers on participatory practice —
the canonical ecosystem reference. Source:
[kunsten.be — participatory art practices](https://www.kunsten.be/en/now-in-the-arts/participatieve-kunstpraktijken-in-vlaanderen-een-actueel-overzicht/),
[kunsten.be — contemporary dance dossier](https://dossiers.kunsten.be/dance/contemporary-dance-flanders-and-brussels).

## How peers handle the web (⚠️ comparative interpretation)

- **Structured production + agenda as the spine.** Peers present a *catalogue of works* with
  per-production pages (credits, media, tour dates) and a real agenda — not image-only posters.
  Leon's image-only `/agenda` is the clearest gap vs. the field.
- **Archive is identity.** les ballets C de la B and Ultima Vez treat the chronological
  archive as a core asset. Leon has rich Gallery photos but no structured work/archive.
- **Localisation, not mixed-language.** Peers offer proper language versions (NL/EN, sometimes
  FR), not trilingual-inline. Leon's mixed-language model is an outlier.
- **Participation as a first-class section.** Ultima Vez gives participatory ateliers their own
  structured area with schedules and sign-up — directly transferable to Leon's Atelier/Mariage.
  ⚠️ *Refined by peer-site triangulation:* even Ultima Vez's *Atelier Quartier* is "free,
  weekly, no registration, just come" — a showcase page, not a sign-up funnel; across all four
  peers participation is presented, never enrolled-via-site. See
  [What Is the Website Actually For?](11-website-purpose.md) → peer triangulation.
- **Custom/managed CMS over basic site builders.** ⚠️ Peer sites read as bespoke or
  CMS-managed, not Squarespace; appropriate for a recurring agenda and multilingual content.

## How peers are funded (context for Leon's gap)

Established peers hold **Kunstendecreet structural *werkingssubsidies*** (per the 2023–2027
[CJM table](https://www.vlaanderen.be/cjm/sites/default/files/2022-06/tabel-beslissing-werkingssubsidies23-27-kunsten-kunstorganisaties.pdf)):
**Ultima Vez €1,040,387/yr**, KVS €3,379,200/yr, Damaged Goods €770,315/yr, MetX €471,040/yr,
Kunstenwerkplaats €405,861/yr, Platform-K €295,536/yr. **Leon has none.** Confirmed (browser
dossier + subsidieregister): Leon runs on **project/short-cycle money only** — Bovenlokale
Cultuurwerking €84,250 (2023), Polsslag Brussel €88,000 (2023)/€78,000 (2025), VGC meerjarig
**€14,840/yr** — i.e. roughly **one to two orders of magnitude below** the structurally-funded
field (its closest lineage peer Ultima Vez is ~70× larger on the Flemish line alone). See
[Organisation Operating Model](21-organisation.md#funding-model) → Funding. Implication: the new
site carries a real **legitimacy/positioning job** — present Leon at peer credibility level for
future structural-funding applications and for partner/programmer trust, without overstating
its current scale.

## Cross-sector comparator (funding-ecology, not artistic peer)

The peers above are *artistic* peers. A separate, sharper comparator is **Debateville** — a
Brussels youth-debate vzw with a **near-identical funding ecology** (project/foundation/public
portfolio: King Baudouin Foundation, VGC, Flanders, Brussels City; no single structural line)
that also reaches its beneficiary **offline** and has built its site explicitly as an
impact/traction instrument — *including* the funder/press/impact **portal** the four dance
peers don't run. It makes the "peer credibility level" legitimacy job above **concrete** and
independently corroborates [What Is the Website Actually For?](11-website-purpose.md). Full
analysis: [Cross-sector comparator — Debateville](27-comparator-debateville.md).
