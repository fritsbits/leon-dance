## 1. The strategic driver: Kunstendecreet 2028–2032

The single most consequential new fact. In Leon's own words:

- *"1 december is de in[dien]date voor het kunst[en]decreet voor structurele subsidies voor de
  periode 28 tot 32. … Die hebben we niet, maar die willen we wel."* — Leon has **no**
  Kunstendecreet structural funding (confirms the registry finding in
  [Organisation](21-organisation.md#funding-model)) and is **actively bidding for it**.
- **Timeline:** dossier in by **1 Dec 2026**; jury assesses **≈ Jan–Jul (Jun) 2027**.
- *"als die eens gaan klikken op de website … dat is een beetje ons uitgangsbord op dat
  moment."* — the site is the calling card the jury will click through to.
- Therefore the redesign's centre of gravity is **showing impact** — *"we doen veel, we zijn
  goed bezig, dit doet ertoe"* — for that jury, **alongside** "where/when/what can I join" for
  participants.

⚠️ Implication: there is now a **de-facto deadline**. The site should be credible to a
structural-funding jury during **Jan–Jul 2027**, which (working back through content,
translation and QC) makes the **beleidsplan window — decisions by ~Sept/Oct 2026, plan due
December 2026** — the real planning anchor. This resolves the long-standing "is structural
funding an explicit ambition the site should support?" question across
[Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md), [Organisation](21-organisation.md),
[Desk Research](10-desk-research.md) with a hard **yes** and a date.

## 2. Who the site is for — client-validated, with a refinement

The covert actor-card exercise tested
[What Is the Website Actually For?](11-website-purpose.md). Outcome: **core claim confirmed,
with one substantive correction and one addition.**

**Confirmed (desk research right):**

- The **hardest-to-reach / most vulnerable groups** Leon works with do **not** use the site —
  reached "via een groep of via een organisatie" (MUS-E, Ketmet, partner orgs), in the street,
  via flyers. A card was explicitly placed under **"Nope."**
- **Programmers & cultuurhuizen**, **funders/policy/researchers**, **press & broad public**
  are genuine site audiences (cards placed, uncontested).
- Existing participants who already know Leon need only **practical info** (time / place /
  who) — the site is not a discovery channel for them.

**Refinement (desk research too absolute):** the desk research argued the site is essentially
*not* for the participant audience. The client corrects this:

- **"Mensen uit de buurt"** was re-read on the card as **potential *new* participants** — people
  triggered **offline** (a fair, a flyer, seeing Leon in public space, an article) who then
  **look the site up to decide "is this for me?"**. For them the site is a **moment of truth**:
  needs were annotated as *snappen "wat"? + is het voor mij? + praktische info*. The current
  site fails them — it opens with mission/vision and they "haken af".
- An **explicit organisational goal** is *more people into the existing ateliers* (not more
  ateliers — see §3). The site is "een puzzelstukje" in that funnel: not the discovery step,
  but the **conversion/reassurance** step after the offline trigger.

⚠️ Net effect on the thesis: *discovery stays offline; the binary "the site is not the
participant funnel" softens to "the site is the participant **moment-of-truth**, not the
discovery channel."* This is a real correction, not a footnote — it changes the home page and
the success metric. Tracked back into [website-purpose](11-website-purpose.md) → "What would
change our mind".

**Addition (a segment desk research missed):** **(freelance) artists & medewerkers** were
added as an audience the cards were missing — an **internal** audience needing *verbinding /
rode draad* (a connective backbone). See §6 (backstage).

**Client's own framing — not a single primary audience (in dialogue).** Kristin: Leon has
**two evenwaardige** publics — the arts sector (consumers, programmers, subsidisers) **and** the
mission audience (people outside the "classic art" group). The site must work for **both**:
the arts/funder side *funds* and *bridges to* the mission side. The agreed problem is not
"pick one" but **rebalance**: the site is currently written *"in bijna subsidiedossiertaal"*,
which buys the money but loses potential participants — *"deze mensen raakten daardoor wel
kwijt. Dus dat evenwicht is wel belangrijk. Zeker in tone of voice en in beeld."*

## 3. Organisational goal: deepen, don't multiply

- *"weet ik niet of we per se staan te springen om nog meer van die trajecten te hebben … we
  zijn eigenlijk met te weinig om … het aantal activiteiten nog te vermenigvuldigen."* The
  goal is to make existing activities **more accessible / more visible** and get **more of the
  right people into the existing ateliers** — **not** to grow the number of
  ateliers/projects.
- ⚠️ Live, **undecided** internal strategy tension: concentrate the *basiswerking* on **one
  place** vs. keep the multi-**antenne** model (antennes in Molenbeek, Sint-Gillis,
  Anderlecht, Koekelberg — an "antenne" = a cluster of culture + welfare + care + education
  partners around one location). Pull both ways: the Kunstendecreet may push focus; **VGC**
  (Brussels) rewards showing presence **across many Brussels municipalities**. Relevant to how
  the site presents locations/reach (and to the impact map idea, §9).

## 4. Communications model — no social media (principled)

Closes [Site Audit](20-site-audit.md) §"Social & external presence" (was "owned socials
inconclusive — confirm with client"):

- *"het enige online dat wij doen is de website. Wij zitten niet op sociale media."* The
  website is the **only** owned online channel. Other channels: **flyers + word of mouth +
  physical presence**; flyers carry the **site URL + a QR code**.
- The no-social stance has **two reasons**: (a) no time/capacity to run it; (b) a **deliberate
  ethical position** — internal critique of social media's mental-health impact (esp. youth);
  Leon "creëert ontmoeting" / real-life connection instead.
- Openness exists to a **passive** social presence (an account that only points back to the
  site / lets people tag Leon, not actively maintained) — unresolved, flagged as part of a
  bigger communication plan, not site scope.

⚠️ Consequence: with no social funnel, the site carries the **entire** owned-channel load —
sharpening the "discoverability hub" job in [website-purpose](11-website-purpose.md).

## 5. The agenda — confirmed mechanics and purpose

Resolves the cluster of agenda open questions (across
[Site Audit](20-site-audit.md), [25-agenda](25-agenda.md),
[26-agenda-analysis](26-agenda-analysis.md), [90-first-impression-review](90-first-impression-review.md)):

- **How it's made:** built in **Excel**, a **screenshot** is taken and **pasted** into
  Squarespace. Updating it means cross-referencing a *separate* planning Excel. Renders badly
  across desktop/mobile. *"een toekomst van inefficiëntie"* with no time to fix it. Inherited
  from the previous comms person; **maintained by Kristin** (comms).
- **Who it's for:** explicitly a **mix** — (a) showing everything Leon does (impact, for
  subsidisers), (b) participants finding where/when to join, (c) **internally**, freelance
  artists check the website for the rehearsal planning (*"Kijk maar op de website"*) — an
  informal internal source of truth, though they're normally briefed via mail/WhatsApp.
- **Problem named by the client:** the agenda conflates *"showing impact"* with *"where can I
  as a participant actually join"*, and mixes open sessions with closed class groups outsiders
  can't join, and changes shape period to period (e.g. 5 weeks of summer stages). ⚠️ → argues
  for **two surfaces**: a structured participant-facing agenda *and* an impact view
  (map/"where we work", cf. Debateville — §9).

## 6. Backstage / internal tooling

A larger theme than the desk research anticipated. Frederik introduced the **frontstage /
backstage** lens (the site is the visible service; the delivery sits behind it; digital tools
can serve both).

- **Pain:** many part-time people and freelancers; coordination scattered across **mail /
  WhatsApp / Excel**; *"interne communicatie is ook een werkpunt."*
- **Wants surfaced:**
  - A **participant database / contact management** centralised across projects and ateliers.
    Today there is **no registration of names** at open ateliers or for people met in public
    space → Leon **loses contact**; newsletter goes out only 3–4×/yr and isn't aimed at new
    participants. Want: site sign-up → contact lands in a DB → Leon can follow up; freelance
    artists could also enter new people's details on the spot.
  - **Owned sign-up** for projects/creations (e.g. Mariage) replacing the external Microsoft
    Forms ([Site Audit](20-site-audit.md) problem #3) — confirmed direction.
  - Possibly a **freelance-team backstage**: a connective function so artists who each run
    separate ateliers can share how sessions went (the Debateville mentor-form model — Frederik
    is a reserve mentor there). Speculative, flagged as *"niet oninteressant"*.
- **Notion-as-source-of-truth hypothesis.** Debateville runs everything (draaiboeken,
  participants, contacts) in **Notion** — flexible, self-managed, hard to replicate as bespoke
  *maatwerk*. Open hypothesis Frederik will research: **couple Notion to the website with
  Notion as the data source** (e.g. enter an event/atelier in the planning → it auto-appears
  in the site agenda). Not yet a recommendation — Frederik flagged that prescribing tooling
  before understanding needs is exactly the anti-pattern from his prior software-selection
  practice.
- **Autonomy vs. dependency — an explicit client requirement.** Leon wants **maximum
  self-sufficiency** (*"dat we effectief niet voor alles moeten bellen"*) and is wary of a
  build that needs Frederik for every change, given the org is still evolving. Trade-off named:
  Squarespace = more manual work but independent vs. custom = more powerful but dependency +
  fragility risk. → drives the editable-content-type approach in §8.

## 7. Multilingual — practical shortcut rationalised as values

Resolves "trilingual: values, shortcut, or both?" ([90-first-impression-review](90-first-impression-review.md),
[Site Audit](20-site-audit.md), [Technical Context](24-technical-context.md)):

- The **mixed-language** model came from advice ("Jeroen": 2–3 languages ≈ building 2–3 sites)
  — a **time/resource choice**, later presented on the site *as if* a strategic stance. The
  client concedes it is *"deels praktisch"* and *"je sluit mensen uit"* (French-only visitors
  drop off fastest; NL readers cope because they often have some EN/FR).
- **Decision:** the new site does **proper localisation**. Frederik builds in **one language
  (likely NL)**, gets content right, then **AI-translates to FR/EN (~95%)**; **Leon
  proofreads** FR/EN. Mission/vision already exists in three languages. Trilingual stays a
  **requirement** but properly localised, not mixed-language inline.

## 8. Build approach, platform, cost, maintenance

Resolves most of [Technical Context](24-technical-context.md) open Qs 1–3:

- **Who built/maintains today:** the Squarespace site was set up by *"Jeroen"* (or Jeroen's
  partner) for a previous comms iteration; content/agenda now maintained by **Kristin**. Small,
  non-technical capacity — the editorial-capacity SWOT risk
  ([Desk Research](10-desk-research.md)) is **confirmed real**: the client raised QC/maintenance
  load themselves and tied scope discipline to it.
- **Cost (current):** Squarespace ≈ **€20–25/mo** (Frederik looked it up; ~15% discount) ≈
  ~€250/yr + hosting ~€200 + the `.dance` domain. **Corroborates the ⚠️ estimate** in
  [Technical Context](24-technical-context.md#running-cost-estimated) (≈ €230–340/yr all-in).
- **Stay on Squarespace?** Not required — the client is open to leaving. Frederik's stated
  plan: build a **custom site he programs himself**, hosted on his server, **similar recurring
  budget**, **replacing** Squarespace (not additive). Design + build are **free** (Frederik
  volunteers).
- **Editability model (the autonomy answer):** structured **content-types Leon can edit**
  (e.g. a "project/creatie" type: title, subtitle, quote, sign-up form, media) + **fixed
  pages** Frederik writes (mission/vision). Mirrors the "harmonie" site he is finishing
  (some pages client-editable, some fixed). Project content likely client-editable in all
  languages; long-form pages translated by AI + client proof.
- **No hard launch date** was set, but the **Kunstendecreet beleidsplan (Dec 2026) / jury
  (Jan–Jul 2027)** is the de-facto deadline; the client wants choices made **between now and
  Sept/Oct 2026**, then the work, "stap voor stap", to stay efficient.

  validation for press + funders (Debateville pattern).
- **Quotes / social proof** — Leon holds many participant & partner-org quotes (gathered for

  potential participants. ⚠️ Confirms the *qualitative-led* proof steer in

- **Impact section** — a "where we work" **map** + impact figures (Debateville pattern). ⚠️
  Caveat the client raised: subsidy-dossier numbers are written **per funder** (VGC = stress
  Brussels; Vlaanderen = don't read as Brussels-only) — impact figures must be **chosen
  carefully**, not lifted verbatim from a dossier.

**Legitimacy / history:** the client is open to a **historiek** in "Who We Are" (*"Where we
come from"*). Key framing line: *"We zijn hier maar vijf jaar bezig. We zijn eigenlijk al 16
