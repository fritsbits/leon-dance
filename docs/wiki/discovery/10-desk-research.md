## Headline picture

Leon vzw is the participatory dance practice around choreographer **Seppe Baeyens** (grown out
of **Ultima Vez**; named after dancer Leon Gyselinck, 1923–2024). Strong, research-grounded
identity (mission/vision, six values, the *Inviter/Mixing/Reversing/Affirming* method) and a
dense Brussels partner network. The **content is strong; the website is the weak link**: a
Squarespace site with an **image-only agenda**, **no real multilingual system**, off-platform
Microsoft-Forms sign-ups, no structured work/archive, and no team/news/pro section. The site
underperforms the peer field (Ultima Vez, les ballets C de la B, kabinet k, fABULEUS).

## What works (keep)

- A clear, distinctive, research-backed positioning and value set — genuinely differentiated.
- Rich photographic documentation (58 credited photos) — a reusable identity asset.
- Strong, legible partner/funder ecosystem and an active programme (Atelier, Mariage, schools).
- Mission/vision already written in **NL/FR/EN** — portable content.

## Biggest problems (drive the redesign)

1. **Image-only agenda** — no dates-as-data, no booking links, inaccessible, invisible to SEO.
2. **No multilingual system** — mixed-language inline + 3 hand-kept mission pages; bad for each
   monolingual audience and for findability.
3. **Off-platform, unowned sign-up** (MS Forms) — no branding, no data, no "who's coming".
4. **No structured content** — events/works/projects are hand-laid sections, not data; every
   update is manual; nothing reusable.
5. **Strong content, weak IA** — method/values buried; no team page (despite ~14 artists), no
   news/press, no programmer/funder ("pro") path.
6. **Discovery is partner-dependent** — no owned social presence linked; visibility relies on
   KVS/KANAL/press, not Leon's own channels.

## SWOT (⚠️ interpretation)

- **Strengths:** distinctive research-based identity; Seppe Baeyens/Ultima Vez lineage; dense
  partner network; strong photo archive; trilingual mission text already exists.
- **Weaknesses:** image-only agenda; no i18n; off-platform forms; no structured content/archive;
  thin IA; unclear owned social presence; small/non-technical maintenance capacity (⚠️).
- **Opportunities:** structured agenda + work archive to reach peer credibility; owned sign-up
  with participant data; proper NL/FR/EN; a "pro/programmer" path; legitimacy for future
  structural-funding (werkingssubsidie) applications.
- **Threats:** low post-launch editorial capacity could strand a richer site; political
  identity (Apartheid Free Zone / BDS) may carry partnership/funding sensitivities (neutral
  flag); over-engineering beyond what the team can maintain.

## Strategic priorities for the redesign (⚠️ proposed, pre-client)

1. Replace the image agenda with a **structured events system** (per-event pages, upcoming/past,
   homepage strip, ticket/partner links).
2. Introduce **proper NL/FR/EN localisation** (locale routing, `hreflang`, switcher).
3. Bring **sign-up in-house** (branded, owned data, exportable lists) — retire MS Forms.
4. Surface the **identity** (mission/values/method) and add **team**, **work/archive**, and a
   **programmer/funder** section.
5. Choose a stack matched to **editorial capacity** — structured content + i18n + forms, not
   another page-builder; final choice is a design-phase decision.

## Recommended site structure (⚠️ first hypothesis)

`Home` · `Agenda` (structured) · `Work/Projects` (Mariage, Atelier Leon, Leon à l'école, White
Page, on Tour + archive) · `Who we are` (identity, method, **team**, Leon Gyselinck) ·
`Participate` (ateliers + Mariage sign-up) · `For partners/press` (dossier, media, contact) ·
`Apartheid Free Zone` (confirm placement with client) · `Contact` — all in NL/FR/EN.

⚠️ **Sharpening (cross-sector comparator).** The
[Debateville comparator](27-comparator-debateville.md) — an org with Leon's exact funding
ecology — suggests two structural upgrades to this hypothesis: (a) make the IA
**audience-first** ("for whom" routes: co-creators / schools / programmers & venues / funders
& partners) rather than internal-programme-first, directly answering problem #5 above; and
(b) add a **Proof/Impact page** (academic method → qualitative evidence → honest output
counts) and an **annual impact-report hub** as the highest-ROI legitimacy moves. Lead the
proof story **qualitatively** (artistic/relational), not with a metrics dashboard — Leon's
value doesn't reduce to survey %s the way a skills programme's does.

## What desk research could not establish

Internal figures (budget, subsidy amounts, headcount, attendance), governance, who maintains
the site, owned social reach, and editorial capacity are all invisible from public sources and
must come from the client. This dossier is a starting point, not a conclusion.
