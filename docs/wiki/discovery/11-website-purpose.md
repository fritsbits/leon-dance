# What Is the Website Actually For?

A foundational framing page: *why does Leon need or have a website, and whom does it serve?*
Later strategy/design decisions should refer back here. Observations plain; interpretations
⚠️; the core conclusion is interpretation built on cited desk-research facts and **must be
validated with the client** (it is open question #1 — see end).

> **Client validation (2026-05-19) — core claim confirmed, one material refinement.**
> The covert actor-card exercise in the [Client Kickoff](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md)
> tested this page. **Confirmed:** the hardest-to-reach core groups are reached **offline**
> (a card was explicitly placed at *"Nope — not a site audience"*); programmers/funders/press
> are real site audiences; the **legitimacy job is now dated and hard** — Leon is bidding for
> **Kunstendecreet structural funding 2028–32** (dossier 1 Dec 2026, jury ≈ Jan–Jul 2027) and
> the site is its *visitekaartje* for that jury. **Refined:** the desk research was too
> absolute in saying the site is *not* for participants. The client insists the site must
> **convert *potential new* participants** — people triggered offline who then look Leon up to
> decide *"is this for me?"* (a **moment of truth**, not the discovery channel) — and an
> explicit org goal is *more people into the existing ateliers*. In the dialogue the client
> was clear the site should **not be reduced to a single audience**: arts-sector/funders and
> the mission audience are "evenwaardig"; the real task is **rebalancing** a site currently
> written in *subsidiedossiertaal*, in tone and image. A new internal audience — **(freelance) artists & staff** — was added. See
> [Client Kickoff §2](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md#2-who-the-site-is-for--client-validated-with-a-refinement).

## The counterintuitive starting point: the site is **not** for Leon's core audience

Leon's mission audience is **neighbourhood participants** (the free "3 to 99" *Atelier Leon*
in Molenbeek/St-Gilles) and **school pupils, incl. special education** (*Leon à l'école*).
These are reached almost entirely **offline** — free, no registration, non-verbal, word of
mouth, via community partners and physical presence — not via `leon.dance`. Source:
[Offerings & Audiences](22-offerings-audiences.md) → "Implications"; [Organisation](21-organisation.md)
→ "How they work".

⚠️ Therefore the website's purpose is **not** "get people to come dance." That conversion
happens in the street and the school. Designing the site as if it were the participant funnel
would optimise for the audience that needs it least.

## Whom the site is actually for: the ecosystem that sustains Leon

⚠️ Interpretation, evidenced. The site exists to serve the audiences Leon **cannot reach in
person but structurally depends on** (full segment list:
[Offerings & Audiences](22-offerings-audiences.md) → "Audience segments"):

1. **Programmers & venues** (KVS, KANAL, BOZAR, De Munt, festivals) who book *Leon on Tour* /
   *Mariage*. Need a professional dossier, tour history, media, contact — **currently absent
   from the site** ([Site Audit](20-site-audit.md): no team/pro/press pages).
2. **Funders & policy/academic stakeholders** (Flemish gov, VGC, Demos, Lasso, researchers).
   Leon is young (incorporated 2021), **project-funded only, no Kunstendecreet structural
   money**, dwarfed by peers (Ultima Vez ≈ €1.04M/yr structural).
   [Organisation Operating Model](21-organisation.md#funding-model). For an org in that
   position the site is a **legitimacy instrument** — proof it is real, methodical,
   research-backed, durable enough to fund.
3. **Press & general public** at the free public-space performances — need clear,
   multilingual "what / when / where".
4. **Co-creators / volunteer participants** for *Mariage* and ateliers — need a frictionless
   sign-up (today offloaded to unbranded external Microsoft Forms;
   [Site Audit](20-site-audit.md) cross-cutting problem #3).

## The concrete jobs that follow

⚠️ Proposed, pre-client — but each maps to a cited gap:

- **Credibility / legitimacy** — surface the distinctive, academically-grounded method
  (*Inviter / Mixing / Reversing / Affirming*, Profs Janssens & Steyaert), today buried in a
  single manual EN page. [Organisation](21-organisation.md) → "Mission/vision/values/method".
- **Bookability** — a programmer-facing dossier: repertoire, tours, the *Birds* → Leon
  credit migration, *Kosmos* 2027, contact. [Organisation Operating Model](21-organisation.md).
- **Discoverability hub** — external discovery is **entirely partner-driven** and Leon has
  near-zero confirmed owned social presence; the site is the one canonical channel Leon
  controls. [Site Audit](20-site-audit.md) → "Social & external presence";
  [Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md).
- **Proof / archive** — the well-populated gallery (58 credited photos) and repertoire are
  evidence assets for funders and press. [Site Audit](20-site-audit.md).
- **Conversion** — newsletter, sign-up, contact: the only owned channels Leon has.

## Why this matters for the brief

⚠️ Designing for *"fill the ateliers"* vs. *"win the next booking and the next subsidy
round"* produces materially different sites (different IA, different home page, different
success metric). The redesign brief should state the primary audience **explicitly** rather
than implicitly serving everyone equally (the current site's failure mode — strong content,
weak IA: [Site Audit](20-site-audit.md) cross-cutting problem #5;
[Desk Research overview](10-desk-research.md)).

## How we know — evidence & triangulation

The claim is counterintuitive, so it was stress-tested five independent ways (desk-research
principle 1: keep proven separate from assumed). All five point the same way.

### 1. Falsification test — what we'd see if the site *were* for the core audience

If the site were the participant funnel we'd expect: atelier schedule front-and-centre,
low-barrier "just come" copy in residents' languages, the agenda as the best-kept page, and
an atelier registration flow. The opposite holds ([Site Audit](20-site-audit.md)): `/agenda` is
**degraded to flat images**, there is **no atelier registration** (participation is
deliberately *free, no registration, drop-in*), and the richest content is the
research-grounded method/values (funder/legitimacy material). Decisive structural point:
**atelier participation requires zero website interaction by design** — a thing that needs no
site cannot be funnelled by one.

### 2. Revealed preference — what Leon actually maintains

What an org keeps polished shows whom it implicitly serves. Leon maintains three hand-kept
trilingual mission pages, a 58-photo *credited* gallery, and a ~35-logo partner/funder footer
(legitimacy + proof artefacts) while letting the agenda rot to images and offloading sign-up
to unbranded MS Forms ([Site Audit](20-site-audit.md); [Organisation](21-organisation.md)). ⚠️
Maintained effort tracks identity/legitimacy, not participant operations.

### 3. Per-segment channel/funnel map

Does the site sit on each audience's path at all? (⚠️ inferred from
[Offerings & Audiences](22-offerings-audiences.md) segments; first-hear channel not yet
client-confirmed.)

| Audience | How they first hear of Leon | What they need | Site on the path? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neighbourhood participants | Community partners, word of mouth, presence | Show up (free, no reg.) | **No** |
| Schools / special-ed | Direct partnerships, teacher networks | Programme fit, contact | Barely (relationship-led) |
| Programmers & venues | Sector network, peers, seeing the work | Dossier, repertoire, media | **Yes — decisively** (unmet) |
| Funders / policy / academic | Sector reputation, applications, partners | Legitimacy, method, evidence | **Yes** |
| Co-creators (Mariage) | The performance/atelier, partners | Frictionless sign-up | **Yes** (today off-platform) |
| Press & public | Partner channels (KVS/KANAL/BRUZZ) | Multilingual what/when/where | **Yes** |

The audiences for whom the site is *decisive* are exactly the non-core ones.

> ⚠️ **Client refinement (2026-05-19):** this table holds for *existing* participants and the
> hardest-to-reach groups (offline — "Nope"). It is **too absolute for *potential new*
> participants**: they hear of Leon offline, then **use the site as a moment-of-truth** to
> decide "is this for me?" — so the site *is* on their path (conversion, not discovery). See
> the banner at the top and [Client Kickoff §2](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md#2-who-the-site-is-for--client-validated-with-a-refinement).

### 4. Peer triangulation — how comparable orgs build their sites

Live-checked four structurally-similar participatory/socially-engaged peers
([Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md) for profiles). Pattern is consistent:

- **[Ultima Vez](https://ultimavez.com/)** (Leon's own incubator/lineage; ≈70× Leon's Flemish
  funding) — nav *Wim Vandekeybus · Ulti'mates (productions) · Participate · Education ·
  Calendar · News · Shop · Contact*. Site built around **productions, calendar, archive**. It
  has the direct analogue to Leon's Atelier — **Atelier Quartier** — and that page states it
  is **"free, weekly, no registration, just come"**
  ([atelier-quartier](https://ultimavez.com/participation/atelier-quartier)). Even a
  structurally-funded peer that could build any sign-up makes its atelier a *descriptive page,
  not a funnel* — independent confirmation of the falsification point.
- **[les ballets C de la B / laGeste](https://www.lesballetscdela.be/en/)** — nav leads
  *Projects → Productions, in co-labo, Workshops*; top-level **Calendar** + **Archive**;
  workshops are showcase pages with **no enrolment flow**; real FR/EN/NL switcher.
- **[kabinet k](https://www.kabinetk.be/)** (closest aesthetic peer) — homepage foregrounds
  latest production *BRON* + "on tour"; Workshops = a menu link with **no schedule/
  registration**; Calendar present; full NL/EN/FR switcher.
- **[fABULEUS](https://www.fabuleus.be/)** — homepage = current productions + news +
  structured venue calendar; participatory items (auditions, volunteers, education) are
  **menu links, not enrolment flows**; archive present.

⚠️ Interpretation: across the category the site's job is a **structured productions +
calendar + archive showcase** for programmers/press/public — participation is a *showcase*,
never a sign-up funnel. Strongly corroborates the core claim. **Honest refinement:** none of
the peers runs an explicit press-kit/funder *portal* either. So for Leon (i) the *table-stakes*
job is the structured production/calendar/archive showcase it currently lacks, and (ii) a
dedicated programmer/funder section would be a **differentiator**, not just catching up — a
sharper read of the "bookability" job above.

### 5. Cross-sector triangulation — Debateville (same funding ecology, different sector)

The four peers above are *artistic* peers. **Debateville** (Brussels youth-debate vzw) is a
**funding-ecology twin**: project/foundation/public portfolio with no single structural line,
beneficiary reached **offline**, same Brussels public layer (King Baudouin Foundation, VGC,
Flanders, Brussels City). Its site is built **explicitly** as an impact/funder/partner/
recruit instrument — homepage metric band, a 3-tier evidence ladder, an annual impact-report
hub, a tiered funder wall, audience-first IA ("Voor wie…"), and owned recruitment funnels for
mentors/co-creators (never for core beneficiaries). Two consequences for the thesis: (a) it is
**independent, cross-sector confirmation** that an org in Leon's exact position builds the
site for the sustaining ecosystem, not the offline-reached core; (b) crucially, Debateville
**does** run the funder/press/impact *portal* the four dance peers don't — so the "honest
refinement" above upgrades: such a portal is a **proven move for a project-funded org seeking
durability**, not merely speculative differentiation. Full analysis:
[Cross-sector comparator — Debateville](27-comparator-debateville.md).

## What would change our mind — resolved by the client (2026-05-19)

> **Status is authoritative in the [Discovery Concerns register](01-concerns.md)** —
> IDs **D-01, D-02**. This section is the local detail/evidence; read status there.

The disconfirming questions below were put to the client (covert form) in the
[Client Kickoff](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md). Outcome:

1. *Did the website play a role when you last won a booking/subsidy renewal?* → The site is
   explicitly positioned as the **Kunstendecreet jury's calling card** for the 2028–32 bid —
   the legitimacy/funder job is **confirmed and dated**, not refuted.
2. *Who contacts you through the site / what do analytics say?* → No analytics discussed, but
   discovery is **offline**: flyers + QR + word of mouth + physical presence; **no social
   media at all** (by principle). The site is the only owned channel — discoverability-hub
   job confirmed.
3. *How do recent participants find you — site vs. partners/word of mouth?* → **Partners /
   street / offline** for the core and hardest-to-reach groups (a card placed at "Nope").

**Net:** the core claim is **not refuted — it is confirmed and sharpened**, with one honest
correction carried into the banner above: the site *is* a **conversion/moment-of-truth step
for potential *new* participants** (post-offline-trigger), and the org wants the site to help
*fill the existing ateliers*. So "the site is not the participant funnel" → "the site is not
the participant **discovery** channel, but it **is** their **moment of truth**." This changes
the home page and the success metric. Residual open items now live in
[Client Kickoff → Open questions](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md#open-questions--status-after-this-meeting).
