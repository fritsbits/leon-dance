# Discovery — Concerns Register

The **single authoritative status index** for the Discovery phase (see
[`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) → "Concerns register (per phase)"). Every open question,
⚠️ assumption, risk or gap is one **deduplicated** concern with a stable ID (`D-nn`). Per-doc
"Open questions" sections are *local detail*; **this page owns the status**. Laid out
**open-first** — the unresolved list is the point of the page; resolved items are an audit
trail at the bottom.

**Markers:** 🔴 Open · 🟡 Partly (partially resolved, remainder named) · ✅ Closed.

## How to use

- **To see what's left:** read the [Unresolved table](#unresolved--what-still-needs-resolving-10).
  That's the page's job. Ignore the audit trail unless you need *why* something closed.
- **On ingest:** when a source resolves a concern, move its row from Unresolved to the audit
  trail with a resolution + pointer; add new concerns at the next free `D-` number (never
  renumber). Refresh the at-a-glance counts.
- **On lint:** reconcile per-doc "Open questions" against this register (statuses agree;
  every per-doc item maps to an ID; no silent Open if concluding the phase).
- **Concluding Discovery → Strategy:** the Unresolved rows seed the Strategy phase's own
  register (`strategy/0x-concerns.md`, created when that phase starts — not before).
