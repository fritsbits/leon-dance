# Offerings & Audiences

What Leon offers, and who each offer serves. Offer facts from `leon.dance/what-we-do`,
`/mariage`, `/mariage-doemee`, `/apartheid-free-zone`. Audience segments are ⚠️ inferred from
offer + partners + public coverage (no client input, no analytics yet).

## The offer

| Offer | What it is | Primary served | Cadence / model |
|-------|------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Atelier Leon** | Free participatory neighbourhood dance workshop, "3 to 99", no experience or registration | Local residents of all ages/backgrounds; incl. people with disabilities; newcomers | Weekly: Sat 10–12 Maison des Cultures Molenbeek; Wed 16–18 Pianofabriek St-Gilles |
| **Leon à l'école** | Dance workshops embedded in school curricula (socio-emotional skills, citizenship, language) | Schools, pupils, teachers — incl. **special education** | Per-school programmes; at Cardijnschool dance became a fixed weekly subject ([jefvandamme.be](https://www.jefvandamme.be/bewegingsateliers-op-school-met-een-topchoreograaf/)) |
| **Mariage** | Flagship participatory performance: open public-space workshops → collective performance; participants shape choreography/scenography/costumes | Co-creators (anyone) + general public/passers-by | Touring editions 2024–2026 (BRONKS/Volt, Cultureghem, MolenFest, GC De Platoo). **Free, no reservation**; sign-up via external MS Forms |
| **Leons White Page** | Open framework making space for "new needs and desires"; social cohesion | Partners + communities | Ongoing initiative |
| **Apartheid Free Zone(s)** | Both an inclusive-space programme *and* a political values commitment (see below) | Whole organisation + partners | Continuous process |
| **Leon rond de tafel** | Monthly coordination + peer-learning gatherings | Internal team + close collaborators | Monthly at Lion City |
| **Leon on Tour** | Partnership interventions: open workshops → large productions | Partner venues/festivals + their publics | Per partnership |

## "Mariage" — clarified

A "celebration of connection through dance and new traditions": weeks of open ateliers in
public space culminating in a participatory performance where the public is taken along.
Free, no reservation. Participation registration currently via **two external Microsoft Forms**
links (one per event), bilingual NL/FR. Source: `leon.dance/mariage`, `/mariage-doemee`.

## "Apartheid Free Zone" — clarified

A prominent top-level identity statement: explicit **solidarity with Palestine and Lebanon**;
a commitment to "consciously avoid any collaborations with states, partners, or residencies
complicit in apartheid regimes," with ongoing review of existing partnerships; links to
`bdsmovement.net`. Issued under organisational authority (no individual signatories). Source:
`leon.dance/apartheid-free-zone`. ⚠️ Observation only — but its prominence is a deliberate
political identity choice with potential partnership/funding sensitivities; a neutral client
discussion point, not a recommendation. (Note: peer *les ballets C de la B* has a comparable
public Palestine strand — see [Ecosystem](23-ecosystem.md).)

## Audience segments (inferred — **now client-validated 2026-05-19**)

> The covert actor-card exercise in the [Client Kickoff](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md)
> validated these six segments and **added**/sharpened: split **existing** participants
> (need only practical info) from **potential *new*** participants (the site's moment-of-truth
> audience); broadened "schools" to **responsible-persons for groups from welfare/education**
> incl. disability orgs; added an **internal (freelance) artists & staff** audience
> (*verbinding / rode draad*); and placed the **hardest-to-reach core groups at "Nope" —
> reached offline, not a site audience**. See
> [Client Kickoff §2 & §10](12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md#2-who-the-site-is-for--client-validated-with-a-refinement).

1. **Neighbourhood participants (core).** Children → seniors ("3 to 99"), all backgrounds, in
   Molenbeek/St-Gilles; explicitly people who feel excluded from or lack access to art &
   culture; newcomers; people with disabilities. Channel: physical presence, word of mouth,
   community partners. Motivation: belonging, play, connection, no barriers (free, no
   registration, non-verbal). Source: [bredeschoolmolenbeek.be](https://www.bredeschoolmolenbeek.be/nl/activiteiten/2021_2022/schooljaar_2021_2022/atelier_leon_(beweging)_met_seppe_baeyens_&_co-2066.html),
   `leon.dance`.
2. **Schools & educators.** Mainstream + **special education** (BUSO Cardijnschool, Zonnelied,
   De Windroos). Teachers/coordinators are the decision-makers/bookers; pupils are end
   participants. Motivation: socio-emotional development, inclusion, curriculum fit. Source:
   [jefvandamme.be](https://www.jefvandamme.be/bewegingsateliers-op-school-met-een-topchoreograaf/).
3. **Cultural-sector partners & programmers.** Venues/festivals (KVS, KANAL, BOZAR, De Munt,
   BRONKS, Cultureghem, MolenFest, STUK) booking *Leon on Tour* / *Mariage*. Need a
   professional dossier, tour history, media, contact — currently absent from the site.
4. **Co-creators / volunteer participants** for Mariage and ateliers — want to join, build a
   temporary community; need a frictionless sign-up.
5. **Funders & policy/academic stakeholders.** Flemish government, VGC, Demos, Lasso,
   Brussels2030; KU Leuven/VUB researchers (method is research-grounded). Need legitimacy,
   impact evidence, reports.
6. **General public & press.** Audiences at free public-space performances; media (e.g.
   [BRUZZ](https://www.bruzz.be/en/culture/theatre-dance/seppe-baeyens-yassin-mrabtifi-and-martha-balthazar-dance-birds-fly-2021-07-06)).
   Need clear, multilingual "what/when/where".

## Implications (⚠️ interpretation → feeds synthesis)

- The two hardest-to-reach core audiences (neighbourhood participants, special-ed pupils) are
  reached **offline**, not via the site — so the site mainly serves *partners, programmers,
  funders, press, and co-creators*. The redesign brief should be explicit about which audience
  the website is actually for. → Developed in
  [What Is the Website Actually For?](11-website-purpose.md).
- A single mixed-language site underserves each monolingual audience; segments 3–5 especially
  expect professional, structured, multilingual information that the current site lacks.
- **Operational corroboration:** the live agenda (Mar–May 2026, 65 entries) confirms 4 of
  these 6 offers with dated evidence and shows *Mariage* is the de-facto flagship by volume
  (~57% of sessions), recurring Atelier/à-l'école the rest, and only 2/65 entries public —
  partners (MUS-E, Ketmet, BRONKS, RITCS) are the recruitment channel, not the site. Full
  analysis: [What the Agenda Reveals](26-agenda-analysis.md) (from
  [Agenda — Extracted Text](25-agenda.md)). White Page, Apartheid Free Zone and *rond de
  tafel* do not appear in the agenda window.
