## P2 · de jury / subsidiënt — the big win (B1)

- **The actual rubric.** A werkingssubsidie dossier is scored on 4 criteria: (1) quality of
  the proposed werking *via the functie-specific criteria*; (2) quality of zakelijk beheer
  (realistic budget · balanced/diverse bestuursorgaan · fair-practice HR); (3) relevance to
  the field/landscape; (4) execution of Strategische-Visienota aandachtspunten. Artistic +
  business are **integrated, not traded off**. · Handleiding 23–27 ch.5 p.23 · High ·
  ⚠️ structure durable, aandachtspunten will change for 28–32.
- **Participatie functie — the function Leon fills** (client `:255`, Leon-specific) — is
  *defined* and scored on three things: **(a)** concept/method + its *meerwaarde voor het
  veld*; **(b)** process — recruitment/selection, artist↔participant co-creation, **degree
  of involvement, aftercare ("nazorg"), continuity of engagement**; **(c)** artistic
  quality of the result **and its evolution over the years**. · Handleiding §6.4 · High.
- **The decree itself names a website-class asset as proof.** The Handleiding states the
  org must have made good-governance/fair-practice work **publicly known via its own
  communication channels** (verbatim; long-term-track clause). · p.24 L1272-1280 · High ·
  ⚠️ clause scoped to the two-period track — strong support, not a blanket rule. → grounds
  G1 ("site = visitekaartje") in the decree, not just inference.
- **Known failure mode = legibility, not effort.** OKo position paper: assessor expertise
  on participation "zeer ongelijk verdeeld"; participatory work risks an "onrechtvaardige
  vergelijkingsbasis" when read as production. · overlegkunsten.org · High · ⚠️ analogue
  (sector-wide, post-2017). → P2 needs the participatory logic in the decree's **shared
  language**, explicitly owning the participatie functie.
- Per-org adviezen are **not public** → no citeable per-peer praise/criticism. · High.
- Durability lever: **"16 not 5 years"** (practice via Ultima Vez: Tornar/INVITED/Birds);
  client wants a *historiek* for young-org credibility · transcript `:603-615` · High ·
  Leon. Jurisdiction trap: VGC wants "in Brussel", Vlaanderen must not read Brussels-only ·
  `:655-661` · High · Leon.

## P4 · de groepsverantwoordelijke — net-new

- **"Antenne" = a cross-sector *cluster*** (culture+welfare+care+education) around a place,
  not a venue — Molenbeek, Sint-Gillis, Anderlecht, Koekelberg · transcript `:665-667` ·
  High · Leon (VQ). Demand posture **constrained** (may *concentrate*, not multiply) ·
  `:267` → P4 site job = *qualify/explain a deliberately constrained model*, not lead-gen.

  *counts* (Cardijnschool put "Leon" on report cards) · lasso.be · High · Leon. The
  teacher/carer **is a participant, not a spectator** · mus-e.be · ⚠️ analogue.
- It is **Leon-initiated, not an application**: Leon maps orgs → invites → "come and taste"
  before commitment · demos.be · High · Leon.
- Who pays (blended): projectsubsidies + school sometimes pays + GC top-up; goal = the
  hardest-to-reach don't pay · transcript `:249-259` · High · Leon.
- **Citeable partner-voice proof** (gatekeeper perspective, Leon-specific, High): Schoemaker
