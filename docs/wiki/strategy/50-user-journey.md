# Strategy — User Journey

> **Ownership (anti-overlap contract).** This page owns the **sequence over time**
> (current→future, friction, intervention). The **person** → [Personas](20-personas.md);
> the **job** → [Jobs-to-be-done](30-jobs-to-be-done.md); the **promise** →
> [Value proposition](40-value-proposition.md). Reference the promise/RTBs by link;
> **don't re-list** them here.

**Bounded staged arcs** for the **three highest-stakes paths — one per goal** (scope
decision **D-v**: map J1/J2/J3 only; note the rest — *no service-blueprint sprawl*). Each
arc = a short ordered set of **moments** with *what happens & feels · current friction ·
future move* (the move **links** to its [VP](40-value-proposition.md) RTB — not re-listed,
per the ownership contract), plus the single **make-or-break moment** and **entry/exit**.

## J1 · Newcomer moment-of-truth — P1 → [G2](10-organisation-goals.md) (everyday primary)

| Moment | What happens & feels | Current friction | Future move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trigger** (offline) | Sees Leon (flyer/QR/public space/article); curious, unsure | — (offline; fine) | Flyer/QR points to a site that won't repel |
| **Land** (cold, homepage) | "Is this for me?" — scanning, guarded | **Mission/vision wall** → *"haakt af"* | Invitation-forward landing ([VP P1](40-value-proposition.md)) |
| **Orient** | Looks for *people like me* + *"wat kan ik doen?"* | Vague text; no "what can I do" | "mensen zoals jij" social proof + clear offer ([VP P1](40-value-proposition.md)) |
| **★ Decisive** | *"Dit is (n)iets voor mij"* — belonging verdict | **Lost here** | Reassured → continues |
| **Act** | Find practical info / sign up; wants low friction | Off-platform MS-Form / no capture | Clear practical info / owned sign-up → contact in DB ([VP P1](40-value-proposition.md), [G4](10-organisation-goals.md)) |
| **After** | Shows up; later just needs the next detail | Contact lost (no capture) | Follow-up; returning = thin utility mode |

**★ Make-or-break:** the belonging verdict on first scan. **Entry:** offline trigger *only*
(not search/social). **Exit (today):** the mission/vision wall.

## J2 · Jury visitekaartje — P2 → [G1](10-organisation-goals.md) (dated keystone)

| Moment | What happens & feels | Current friction | Future move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Reads dossier/beleidsplan → clicks the site as *visitekaartje*; due-diligence, skeptical | — | The dossier links to a site that *corroborates* (the decree counts own channels as proof) |
| **Land** | "Is this real and at peer level?" | Underwhelms vs structurally-funded peers | Invitation line **+ immediate rigor/durability signal** |
| **Orient** | Scans for the *participatie* case, against the rubric | Method buried on one EN page; no architecture | Deep entry to the proof ladder ([VP P2](40-value-proposition.md)) |
| **★ Decisive** | "Does this evidence the functie — *method-meerwaarde · process/aftercare/continuity · quality-evolution*?" | Case left **implicit** → underwhelms | Each rung **visibly evidenced** |
| **Corroborate** | Cross-checks endorsement & recognition | Flat ~35-logo footer; recognition buried | Tiered funder/partner wall + recognition (⚠️ lineage proxy [S-11](01-concerns.md)) |
| **After** | Leaves convinced it's real, methodical, durable | The site didn't help the dossier | The site *is* corroborating evidence |

**★ Make-or-break:** the rubric-evidenced decisive moment. **Entry:** from the dossier
(Jan–Jul 2027). **Exit (today):** "young / Brussels-only / charity, not rigorous". ⚠️
Rubric = **2023–27** Handleiding (28–32 aandachtspunten not yet public).

## J3 · Programmer booking — P3 → [G3](10-organisation-goals.md)

| Moment | What happens & feels | Current friction | Future move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trigger** | Hears via the sector / sees the work; maybe-bookable interest | — | — |
| **Land** | Goes to the site for a dossier | **No team/pro/dossier** → dead end | A pro/dossier surface exists |
| **Orient** | "What's actually bookable?" — assessing fit/risk | Only *"een paar beelden van die ene keer"*; the bike invisible | Three bookable things — Mariage-as-format · the bike · co-production ([VP P3](40-value-proposition.md)) |
| **★ Decisive** | "Credible & low-risk to programme — and who do I contact?" | Relationship-only; no surface to act on | Track record (*Birds*/*Kosmos*) + clear contact |
| **Act → After** | Reaches the right person → books → tour/co-pro relationship | Ad hoc / stalls | Clear contact path → books |

**★ Make-or-break:** the credibility-and-contact moment. **Entry:** sector / seeing the
work (*not* the site cold). **Exit (today):** no professional surface.

  basics*; a short utility path, event-bound.

- **N · kerngroep** — no journey; not on the site's path ([20](20-personas.md)).

(the engagement is a website redesign; front/back-of-house is a lens, not an ops exercise).
