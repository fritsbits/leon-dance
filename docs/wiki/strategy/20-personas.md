# Strategy — Personas

> **Ownership (anti-overlap contract).** This page owns the **person** — context,
> trigger, motivation, anxiety, behaviour, representativeness, voice. The **job** they
> hire the site for → [Jobs-to-be-done](30-jobs-to-be-done.md); the **promise** →
> [Value proposition](40-value-proposition.md); the **sequence over time** →
> [User journey](50-user-journey.md). Personas cross-link by stable ID (P1–P6, N) and
> **never restate** the job/promise/flow.

Nine actor-cards from the covert kickoff exercise, modelled as people and enriched from
the client transcript + desk research. Tied to the [goals](10-organisation-goals.md).

**Tiering:** *Primary* = the site is decisive · *Secondary* = on the path but
lighter/relationship-led · *Internal* = backstage-adjacent (later/light, per the
website-redesign mandate) · *Non-audience* = named on purpose.

**Modelling decision (D-ii, [brief](00-strategy-brief.md#locked-strategic-decisions)):** the
participant is **one person across journey stages**, not three personas — the stages live
in the [user journey](50-user-journey.md).

**Confidence summary.** P1/P2/P3/P4/N High · P5 High (segment) / Med (depth) · P6 Med.
P2/P3/P5 are desk-ceiling (the participant survey can't reach them — acceptable). P1
(and possibly P4) firmable by [Kristin's survey](01-concerns.md).

## P1 · De nieuwsgierige — *primary, serves [G2](10-organisation-goals.md)* · job → [30 P1](30-jobs-to-be-done.md)

- **Context** — encountered Leon **offline**: a flyer/QR, Leon working visibly in public
  space for weeks, or an article. No social media exists (principled), so the site is the
  *only* place to look Leon up.
- **Trigger → channel** — a concrete offline spark → scans/types the URL → lands cold,
  usually on the homepage.
- **Motivation** — settle one question: *"is dit iets voor mij — mensen zoals ik?"* and
  *"ga ik mij hier op mijn gemak voelen?"*
- **Anxiety / barrier** — fear of not belonging; bounces (*"haakt af"*) when met with
  missie/visie in *subsidiedossiertaal*. **The single thing the site must defuse.**
- **Behaviour** — scans, doesn't read deep; looks for *people like me* + *"wat kan ik hier
  doen?"* + practical info; leaves fast if absent.
- **Representativeness** — the audience [G2](10-organisation-goals.md) is built for;
  reached **only** post-offline-trigger — *not* a discovery channel (the offline core is N).
- **Voice** — **real participant verbatim** (Leon-curated, from the missie/visie page):
  *"I feel welcome and accepted, without judgements. I can be myself there and I meet
  people I would never have otherwise encountered."* · (FR) *"C'est le seul endroit où on
  peut être différent ensemble."* These double as proof-ladder social-proof — see
  [30 proof job](30-jobs-to-be-done.md#the-cross-cutting-decision-the-shared-proof-job) /
  [40](40-value-proposition.md); personas own them **as the person's words**, not as RTB.

## P2 · De jury / subsidiënt — *primary, serves [G1](10-organisation-goals.md)* · job → [30 P2](30-jobs-to-be-done.md)

- **Context** — assessing Leon's **Kunstendecreet 2028–32 structural** dossier (jury reads
  dossiers ≈ Jan–Jul 2027) and clicking the site as the *visitekaartje*. Also
  policy/VGC/Demos/academic readers.
- **What's at stake (for them)** — a defensible allocation of structural money to a **young
  (vzw 2021), project-funded** org ≪ structurally-funded peers. They score the
  **participatie functie** Leon fills on **three defined things**: (a) the participatory
  *method + its meerwaarde voor het veld*; (b) the *process* — recruitment/selection,
  artist↔participant co-creation, **degree of involvement, aftercare ("nazorg"),
  continuity**; (c) *artistic quality of the result and its evolution over the years*.
  See [Research Evidence §P2](90-research-evidence-2026-05-19.md).
- **Motivation** — fast confirmation **against that rubric** that *"dit doet ertoe"*.
  Decisive: the decree **itself treats an org's own communication channels as proof** of
  its commitments — so to this persona the site is *evidence*, not a brochure.
- **Anxiety / barrier** — (i) the **legibility trap** — ⚠️ *analogue (OKo, sector-wide)*:
  participatory work is assessed unevenly and risks an "unfair comparison basis" if read
  as production → Leon must own the *participatie functie* explicitly, in the decree's
  shared language; (ii) the **per-funder framing trap** — VGC wants Brussels stressed,
  Vlaanderen must not read Brussels-only; (iii) **young-org credibility**, countered
  by the **"16 not 5 years"** lineage (practice via Ultima Vez: Tornar/INVITED/Birds) the
  client wants surfaced as a *historiek*.
- **Behaviour** — reads *against the rubric*, not for vibes; clicks deeper and does more
  work than a casual visitor — method, process/aftercare, quality-evolution, governance,
  third-party validation, reports.
- **Representativeness** — tiny in number, **decisive in consequence** (the dated keystone).
- **Voice** — *"Dat zijn uw impactcijfers, dat je moet hebben. Daar komen de subsidies
  van."* — the jury's logic in the client's words.

⚠️ **Freshness note.** The rubric is the **2023–27** Handleiding — the 28–32 Strategische
Visienota/aandachtspunten were not yet public; treat the criteria *structure* as durable,
do **not** hard-wire 23–27 aandachtspunten. Tracked [S-07](01-concerns.md).

## P3 · De programmator — *primary, serves [G3](10-organisation-goals.md)* · job → [30 P3](30-jobs-to-be-done.md)

- **Context** — a programmer/cultuurhuis (KVS, KANAL, De Munt, BRONKS, festivals) weighing
  booking. There are **three bookable things**, not one: (1) ***Mariage* as a replicable
  format** — a fixed core cast + artists tour, each venue adding a *local cast* (~10
  rehearsals → show; 6 editions 2024–26); (2) the **mobiele dansstudio / "the bike"** — a
  bookable summer public-space offer that today has **no digital surface at all** (sold via
  a brochure emailed to past partners), which the client explicitly wants site-bookable;
  (3) **co-production at institutional scale** — *Kosmos* 2027 (KANAL-Centre Pompidou /
  KVS), with *Birds* tour-direction since 2023 as track record. See
  [Research Evidence §P3](90-research-evidence-2026-05-19.md).
- **Motivation** — decide bookability quickly and reach the right person.
- **Anxiety / barrier** — today there is only *"zo'n paar beelden van die ene keer"* and
  no dossier/team/contact — not enough to act on.
- **Behaviour** — relationship-led, but needs a credible professional surface to justify/
  initiate; reads repertoire, tour history, needs, contact.
- **Representativeness** — small, high-value; the booking pipeline.
- **Voice** — ⚠️ *inferred from the client's account (not a programmer's own words):* "I
  saw it once — but where's the dossier to actually book this?"

⚠️ The **bike as a site-bookable offer** is a *Scope/Design* decision — noted here as P3
context, not decided in Strategy.

## P4 · De groepsverantwoordelijke — *secondary, serves [G2](10-organisation-goals.md)/[G3](10-organisation-goals.md)* · job → [30 P4](30-jobs-to-be-done.md)

- **Context** — a responsible person at a partner from **culture / welfare / care /
  education**. An *"antenne"* = a **cross-sector *cluster*** (not a venue) Leon builds
  around a place (Molenbeek, Sint-Gillis, Anderlecht, Koekelberg). Demand posture is
  **constrained**: Leon may *concentrate* a core antenne rather than multiply — so the
  site's P4 job is to **qualify and explain a deliberately limited model**, not generate
  volume. A trajectory is e.g. 6th-graders, **weekly (a fixed weekday), 2h, ~6 months**, a
  relationship built through the work. See
  [Research Evidence §P4](90-research-evidence-2026-05-19.md).
- **Motivation** — understand what a Leon trajectory does *for their group* and that
  *"zij bij ons terecht kunnen daarvoor"*; see the group-mixing model.
- **Anxiety / barrier** — the real fear is **institutional, not artistic**: time,
  whole-team buy-in, and whether it *counts* (the Cardijnschool put "Leon" on pupil report
  cards). The teacher/carer **is a participant, not a spectator** (⚠️ *analogue: MUS-E*).
  It is **Leon-initiated** (Leon maps orgs → invites → "come and taste" before any
  commitment) — P4 does *not* apply. Funding is blended (projectsubsidies + school
  sometimes pays + GC top-up; goal: the hardest-to-reach don't pay — *"altijd samen te
  bekijken"*).
- **Behaviour** — **relationship-led**: the site is reassurance + a low-threshold contact
  start, **not** a funnel.
- **Representativeness** — the recruitment channel that reaches the core groups (partners
  reach N; the site does not).
- **Voice** — **partner-coordinator verbatim** (the gatekeeper's *own* words): Els
  Schoemaker (BUSO Cardijnschool) *"Het atelier is een plek waar je anders kan
  communiceren: met je handen, voeten en hoofd."*; Sarah Dewin (Zonnelied) *"…zien wij als
  begeleiders plots dat mensen dingen kunnen die we niet wisten."* Plus the Leon-curated
  *leerkracht-deelnemer* quote *"…Ik zie mijn leerlingen elke dag in de klas, maar niet op
  deze manier."*

## P5 · Pers & breed publiek — *secondary, serves [G3](10-organisation-goals.md)* · job → [30 P5](30-jobs-to-be-done.md)

- **Context** — around the **rare** free public-space performances (only ~2/65 agenda
  entries are public). Press has covered the **lineage works** (INVITED/Tornar/Birds —
  reviews + het TheaterFestival juried selection), with a reliably **social, not
  aesthetic** angle ("wordless common language", public space).
- **Motivation** — clear multilingual what/when/where; press also wants the basics +
  visuals + a *"anderen schrijven over ons"* third-party-validation framing.
- **Anxiety / barrier** — today the agenda is an image; nothing press-usable.
- **Behaviour** — press *"gaat al wat verder klikken"*; the public wants the practical
  essentials, fast.
- **Representativeness** — episodic (event-bound); broad reach when it happens.
- **Voice** — *(no distinctive client verbatim — evidence-only.)*

⚠️ **Open gap:** *Mariage* (Leon's *own* flagship) has **no traceable published critic
review**; recognition rests on the **lineage as proxy** (INVITED/Tornar het TheaterFestival
selection; no won prizes). Ask the team — there may be Mariage press we missed. Tracked
[S-11](01-concerns.md).

## P6 · De freelance artiest / medewerker — *internal, light, serves [G4](10-organisation-goals.md)/[G5](10-organisation-goals.md)* · job → [30 P6](30-jobs-to-be-done.md)

Kept deliberately short (scope flag). The ~14-strong freelance pool is the operational
backbone (Lena/Stef/Kristin/Adnane…); they don't all meet weekly and **already** check the
site to know *"wat gebeurt er waar en waar word ik verwacht?"* A possible connective
backstage exists as an idea — flagged *niet-oninteressant*, **not built this phase**
([S-06](01-concerns.md)).

## N · De moeilijkst bereikbare kerngroep — *non-audience, by design*

The most vulnerable core groups Leon works with **do not use the site** — reached *"via
een groep of via een organisatie"*, in the street, and through weeks of physical presence
before a public-space show. **No person-profile fields** — by definition off-site.

**Why named (a decision, not an omission):** *not* designing for N is what fixes the
"strong content, weak IA — serves everyone equally" failure mode; it stops the site being
optimised for the audience that needs it least.
