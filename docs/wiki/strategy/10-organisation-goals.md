# Strategy — Organisation Goals

Garrett **product objectives**: what Leon needs *the site* to achieve (website goals, not
generic org goals). Prioritised, minimal overlap. Front door:
[Strategy Brief](00-strategy-brief.md). Confidence scheme defined there
(**C: High/Med/Low · Basis · To validate**; validation gate = client reaction to the
content-complete draft).

## Governing principle (sits above all goals — not itself a goal)

The site must serve the **funder/legitimacy** objective and the **new-participant**
objective **as equals** ("evenwaardig"), rebalanced *out of subsidiedossiertaal* in tone
**and** image. Every goal below is pursued under this constraint.

- **C: High** · **Basis:** [Client Kickoff §2](../discovery/12-client-kickoff-2026-05-19.md#2-who-the-site-is-for--client-validated-with-a-refinement)
  — Kristin's own framing: the arts/funder side *funds and bridges to* the mission side;
  *"deze mensen raakten daardoor wel kwijt … dat evenwicht is wel belangrijk. Zeker in
  tone of voice en in beeld."* · **To validate:** brand/tone assets;
  which proof is safe per-funder ([S-08](01-concerns.md), [S-09](01-concerns.md)).

## How G1 and G2 cohere (the key resolution)

In the kickoff *dialogue* the client was clear the site shouldn't be reduced to a single
audience — yet a site still needs a centre of gravity for IA/home-page decisions. Resolved
**on the time axis, not by ranking** (decision **D-i**, [brief](00-strategy-brief.md#locked-strategic-decisions)):

- **G1 = the dated deadline + credibility bar.** It sets *when* (jury-credible by
  Jan–Jul 2027 → content ~Sept/Oct 2026) and the *quality floor* (peer-credible
  legitimacy). It does **not** claim the home page.
- **G2 = the everyday front door.** The home page and default IA optimise for the
  newcomer's moment of truth.
- They don't compete: one sets the deadline and the credibility bar, the other sets the
  default path. "Evenwaardig" in tone+image is preserved as the governing principle.

This is the strategic resolution of the Discovery recommendation to "state a primary
audience explicitly" ([Website Purpose](../discovery/11-website-purpose.md)) *without*
overriding the client's clear wish (from the kickoff dialogue) not to sideline the mission
audience — and without pretending a real site reduces to a single goal or audience.

## ⚠️ Goal-vs-constraint note

**G4** and **G5** read partly as *constraints* as well as goals. They are kept as goals
because the client raised both as explicit organisational wants and both are load-bearing
for every downstream Scope decision. If the Scope phase prefers an outcomes-only goal list,
G4/G5 can be demoted to constraints there — flagged so the choice is conscious, not lost.
