# Strategy — Organisation Goals

Garrett **product objectives**: what Leon needs *the site* to achieve (website goals, not
generic org goals). Prioritised, minimal overlap. Front door:
[Strategy Brief](00-strategy-brief.md).

## Governing principle (sits above all goals — not itself a goal)

The site must serve the **funder/legitimacy** objective and the **new-participant**
objective **as equals** ("evenwaardig"), rebalanced *out of subsidiedossiertaal* in tone
**and** image. Every goal below is pursued under this constraint. Kristin's framing in the
kickoff: the arts/funder side *funds and bridges to* the mission side; *"deze mensen
raakten daardoor wel kwijt … dat evenwicht is wel belangrijk. Zeker in tone of voice en in
beeld."* Brand/tone assets and which proof is safe per-funder are open
([S-08](01-concerns.md), [S-09](01-concerns.md)).

## The goals

| # | Goal |
|---|------|
| **G1** | **Be a credible *visitekaartje* for the Kunstendecreet 2028–32 jury** — the keystone, *dated*: credible to a structural-funding jury Jan–Jul 2027; content anchor ~Sept/Oct 2026, beleidsplan Dec 2026. **Rubric-grounded:** the jury scores the ***participatie* functie** on *method+meerwaarde · process/aftercare/continuity · quality-evolution*, and the decree **itself counts an org's own communication channels as proof** — so the site isn't just a brochure to the jury, it's *evidence*. |
| **G2** | **Convert offline-triggered *potential new* participants into the *existing* ateliers/creaties** — held co-equal with G1; site = the *moment-of-truth* step, **not** the discovery channel. **Deepen, don't multiply.** |
| **G3** | **Win the next booking** — bookable by programmers/cultuurhuizen: *Mariage* on tour, the *mobiele dansstudio* (bike) as a bookable offer, *Kosmos* 2027 as forward pipeline. |
| **G4** | **Own the channel and the relationship** — the *only* owned channel (no social, principled): bring sign-up + participant contact in-house (retire MS Forms), stop losing contact after offline encounters. |
| **G5** | **Be maintainable by Leon itself** — self-sufficiency requirement: structured editable content-types + AI-translate→client-proof, so a small non-technical comms function runs it without calling Frederik for every change. |

⚠️ **Rubric freshness (G1):** the rubric is the **2023–27** Handleiding — the 28–32
aandachtspunten are not yet public. Treat the criteria *structure* as durable; do **not**
hard-wire 23–27 aandachtspunten. Tracked [S-12](01-concerns.md).

## How G1 and G2 cohere (the key resolution)

In the kickoff *dialogue* the client was clear the site shouldn't be reduced to a single
audience — yet a site still needs a centre of gravity for IA/home-page decisions. Resolved
**on the time axis, not by ranking** (decision **D-i**,
[brief](00-strategy-brief.md#locked-strategic-decisions)):

- **G1 = the dated deadline + credibility bar.** It sets *when* (jury-credible by Jan–Jul
  2027 → content ~Sept/Oct 2026) and the *quality floor* (peer-credible legitimacy). It
  does **not** claim the home page.
- **G2 = the everyday front door.** The home page and default IA optimise for the
  newcomer's moment of truth.
- They don't compete: one sets the deadline and the credibility bar, the other sets the
  default path. "Evenwaardig" in tone+image is preserved as the governing principle.

## ⚠️ Goal-vs-constraint note

**G4** and **G5** read partly as *constraints* as well as goals. They are kept as goals
because the client raised both as explicit organisational wants and both are load-bearing
for every downstream Scope decision. If the Scope phase prefers an outcomes-only goal list,
G4/G5 can be demoted to constraints there — flagged so the choice is conscious, not lost.
