## 3. Mariage is a partnership/co-production engine with a rehearsal→show arc

⚠️ Inferred from the sequence: **March** = dispersed school + partner workshops
(`Mariage in de klas` weekly at BRONKS & RITCS; `Atelier Mariage x MUS-E` at GBS
Kameleon; `x Ketmet` at Cultureghem). **Early–mid April** = intensive consolidation

participatory piece built *with cohorts* then performed locally — ⚠️ the two shows are
likely the **same work performed with two different participant groups/venues**, not

Named recurring partners surface as hard evidence: **MUS-E**, **Ketmet/Cultureghem**,
**BRONKS**, **RITCS**, **Maison des Cultures**, **Pianofabriek**, **De Loods**. This
substantiates [Offerings & Audiences](22-offerings-audiences.md) audience segments **2
(schools/educators)** and **3 (cultural-sector partners)** and the "co-creation with
partner organisations" model in [Organisation](21-organisation.md) — the agenda shows
*which* partners and *at what cadence* (weekly through March), which the offer pages
state only in the abstract.

## 5. Operational footprint & the website-purpose link

**~65 sessions in ~13 weeks ≈ 5/week sustained** — a substantial weekly footprint for
a small org. Implications:

- **Editorial/CMS sizing:** a future system must model **high-frequency recurring
  series + partner-tagged project arcs**, not a handful of one-off shows (feeds
  [Technical Context](24-technical-context.md) and the [Site Audit](20-site-audit.md)
  "who maintains the agenda" question).
- **Audience routing:** with only 2/65 entries public and all workshops recruited
  *through* schools/partner orgs, the agenda is concrete evidence that **participants
  do not reach Leon via the website** — strongly reinforcing
  [What Is the Website Actually For?](11-website-purpose.md): the site serves
  programmers/funders/press/co-creators, while the core participatory audience is
  reached offline through MUS-E, Ketmet, BRONKS, RITCS, Maison des Cultures.
- **Collaboration grammar:** Leon labels work as `X x Y` (`Atelier Mariage x MUS-E`,
  `Leon on tour x Opening De Loods`, `Atelier Leon x FIESTA`). ⚠️ The offer is
  fundamentally relational; the "x partner" framing is identity, not just logistics —
  relevant to how offers should be presented on a redesigned site.
