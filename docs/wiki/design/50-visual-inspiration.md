# Visual Inspiration — UI references

> **Plane** 5 (Garrett, Surface) — **research-collecting, plane not yet opened.** A curated
> library of ~20 sites informing Surface decisions on typography, color, motion, image
> treatment, navigation, multilingual UX, and editorial rhythm. Captured ahead of the
> Skeleton/Surface work so visual direction has source material when those planes start.

## Method

Two sourcing strategies, deliberately picked to avoid the local echo:

1. **International dance peers, ethos-aligned with Leon** — choreographer-led collectives
   doing participatory / intergenerational / community-rooted / nomadic work. Explicit
   bias *away* from BE/NL same-subsector references (Rosas, P.A.R.T.S., Damaged Goods,
   Eastman, Peeping Tom, les ballets C de la B, etc.) — too close to the local scene to
   inspire fresh thinking. We know what they look like; that's context, not inspiration.
2. **Design-gallery "dance" sweep** — Awwwards · SiteInspire · Typewolf · Godly · One Page
   Love · Httpster filtered on "dance" / "performance" / "choreography". Curatorial yield,
   not a pre-named list.

Plus a small set of **non-dance wildcards** covering lenses dance peers don't (multilingual
benchmark, motion-as-craft, editorial/dispatch structure, solo-artist site at scale) and
**3 cultural institutions** with editorial polish — all international, not BE/NL.

### What anchors each reference to Leon

Every entry passes a "fit" check against the Strategy outputs:

- ⚠️ *Visitekaartje for a funding jury* **and** *moment-of-truth for participants* — must
  read as **serious-cultural and warm-participatory** at once. Sites that are only one
  of those miss Leon ([VP / register-pair](../strategy/40-value-proposition.md)).
- ⚠️ *Site is Leon's only owned channel* — references where the site does all the
  storytelling, not where it's a thin shell pointing at Instagram
  ([G4 own-the-channel](../strategy/10-organisation-goals.md)).
- ⚠️ *EN/FR/NL multilingual* — language switching elegant, not bolted on.
- ⚠️ *Maintainability gate* — patterns Frederik can keep alive in Laravel + Kristin can
  edit. Awwwards-bait that needs a designer-on-call doesn't fit
  ([Scope maintainability gate](20-scope.md)).

it's here" · 2–3 bullets *transferable to Leon* · 1 bullet *what NOT to copy* · tags.

## Reference write-ups

### 1. Dance Exchange (Liz Lerman legacy)

![Dance Exchange homepage](../../raw/assets/inspiration/liz-lerman-dance-exchange.png)

**URL:** <https://danceexchange.org> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer ·
intergenerational · community-rooted · register-pair · photo-as-positioning

**Why it's here.** Closest international ethos match for Leon: a US dance organisation
(founded by Liz Lerman in 1976, 45+ years) doing explicitly intergenerational, community-
rooted practice. The home does the *visitekaartje*-AND-*participant* job in one frame —
not by splitting registers across sections.

**Observations.**

- Hero = a single photograph (two older dancers in a community garden, urban backdrop)
  with the wordmark "Dance Exchange" in large white serif and the four-clause mission as
  strapline: *"Expanding who gets to dance, where dance happens, what dance is about, and
  why dance matters."*
- Primary nav: 8 items — *Who We Are · Move at DX · Performance Projects · Training · Rent
  Space · Connect · Support · Search.*
- Logo is a hand-drawn / painted olive-green wordmark — small, top-left, low-key.
- Below the fold: editorial blocks (board chair message, executive director message,
  project announcement), then mission, team, support, partner sections. Partner list
  includes NASA, Kennedy Center, Jacob's Pillow, Harvard Law School — institutional range.
- Body palette = off-white field, dark text, photos doing the colour work.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ *Mission-on-hero-photo as register-pair device.* The photo carries warmth (real
  participants, real ages, no studio body-perfect aesthetic); the four-clause mission
  carries institutional articulation. **Both registers in one frame, no toggle, no
  trade-off.** This is exactly the visitekaartje-AND-participant tension Leon's home has
  to resolve ([VP / register-pair](../strategy/40-value-proposition.md)). The lever isn't
  copywriting cleverness; it's **photo selection discipline**.
- ⚠️ *Strapline as IA armature.* "Expanding WHO · WHERE · WHAT · WHY" reads as a
  manifesto but functions like a content map. Leon's evenwaardig positioning could yield a
  similar four-clause anchor that *also* labels the four primary sections — a
  copy/IA double-duty pattern worth designing for.
- *Editorial below-fold rhythm.* "Message from the Board Chair", "Message from Executive
  Director", project announcement — short signed blocks, dated, low-design. Maintainable
  for Kristin: write a paragraph, hit publish ([Scope maintainability gate](20-scope.md)).
- *Logo restraint.* Tiny, top-left, hand-drawn — refuses to be the brand carrier.
  The *work* (the photo, the mission) carries the brand. Useful counter-pressure if Leon
  drifts toward big-logo solutions.

**What NOT to copy.**

- 8-item primary nav violates Leon's [Structure decision](30-structure.md) of 4 top-level
  items. Don't import the breadth; their categorisation logic is fine, the count is not.
- 4-clause strapline (~22 words) is legible on big-text desktop but unwieldy on mobile
  and as a meta-description. Leon's anchor should be **tighter** — 1–2 clauses max.
- Below-fold typography is generic-nonprofit (default serif body, no rhythm) — Leon can
  push much further on typographic craft without sacrificing accessibility or
  editability.

### 2. Boris Charmatz / [terrain]

![Boris Charmatz / Terrain homepage](../../raw/assets/inspiration/boris-charmatz.png)

**URL:** <https://www.borischarmatz.org> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer ·
mass-amateur · documentary-photography · tour-info-on-hero · French

**Why it's here.** The single most directly Leon-relevant peer in the set. Charmatz's
work (*10000 gestes, Cercles, Levée des conflits*) is built around mass amateur
participation in conceptual choreographic projects. The site's hero photo says everything
the brief says: non-professional bodies, real space, mixed ages, joy-in-rigour, scale.

**Observations.**

- Hero = full-bleed aerial photograph of ~80+ visible bodies of varied ages / sizes /
  clothing in what reads as a large hall. No stage, no lit performance moment.
- Wordmark "Terrain / Boris Charmatz" in large white serif, top-left over the image.
- Right-overlay info panel headed *ACTUALITÉS* with live tour dates: Muelle Création 2026
  · Kunstenfestivaldesarts Brussels (BE) · ImPulsTanz Vienna · Festival d'Avignon ·
  10000 gestes Mannheim · Cercles Lyon.
- Left vertical text nav: *AGENDA · SPECTACLES · ÉVÉNEMENTS / EXPOSITIONS · FILMS ·
  PUBLICATIONS · STUDIO · CHEZ BORIS · À PROPOS · CONTACTS · NEWSLETTER · IMPRINT*.
- French only, no language toggle visible.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ **Photo discipline as the single most important takeaway.** This hero is the
  visual register Leon wants to inhabit. Leon's [image map](90-image-map.md) should be
  screened against this benchmark — *does this photo say what Boris Charmatz's hero says?*
  If not, choose another photo.
- ⚠️ *Live tour-dates in the hero.* "Where you can see this work right now" surfaced
  alongside the brand-mark = visitekaartje (legit) + practical (where + when). Mapping
  onto Leon's [Agenda dual-job](30-structure.md), but at much higher prominence — worth
  considering whether Leon's home elevates next-edition dates similarly.
- ⚠️ *Wordmark calm against documentary-hot photo.* The serif type stays out of the way;
  the photo carries the energy. A workable tension if Leon's wordmark is restrained.
- *Long-form vertical secondary nav* — when the homepage IS the entry (not a wayfinding
  moment), depth can sit quietly in a left rail. A pattern Leon could borrow for archive
  pages rather than primary nav.

**What NOT to copy.**

- Single-language (FR only) doesn't match Leon's EN/FR/NL spec — non-negotiable for our
  context.
- Vertical left nav crowds a small viewport; mobile likely poor. Leon's
  [Structure](30-structure.md) constraints rule this out as primary IA.

### 3. Akram Khan Company

![Akram Khan Company homepage](../../raw/assets/inspiration/akram-khan.png)

**URL:** <https://www.akramkhancompany.net> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer ·
production-driven · choreographer-as-brand · dark-mood · UK

**Why it's here.** Major UK choreographer-led company touring internationally. Useful as
the *opposite pole* to Leon: choreographer-as-brand rather than collective-with-
choreographer. Studying the contrast sharpens what Leon shouldn't import.

**Observations.**

- Hero = two stacked production tiles — top "*THIKRA*", bottom "*HOTTO DESH*" — each with
  a "Find out more" affordance. Production photography, dark warm-brown palette.
- Wordmark "AKRAM KHAN COMPANY" in small white caps top-left.
- Nav top-right, 4 items: *Calendar · Legacy · Productions · About us.*
- EU cookie banner bottom-left.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ *Productions-as-tiles on the hero.* When a company has named works that travel,
  presenting them as parallel entry points (vs. a single hero) handles the "which work
  brought you here?" question elegantly. Maps to Leon's *Mariage* / *Birds* / *INVITED*
  set of named projects (each with editions) — a candidate home-pattern for
  [Structure](30-structure.md)'s *Participatieve performances* section.
- ⚠️ *Tight 4-noun nav.* "Calendar · Legacy · Productions · About us" hits a generic-
  first / brand-second discipline very close to Leon's [Structure](30-structure.md)
  4-item rule. Note "Legacy" (warmer, curatorial) instead of "Archive" — a copy nuance
  Leon can borrow.
- *Cinematic-dark palette* carries gravitas without going corporate-institutional.

**What NOT to copy.**

- Choreographer-IS-the-brand framing — Leon's collective spine
  ([Strategy / VP](../strategy/40-value-proposition.md)) would tilt away from the
  participatory register if imported.
- Dark-mood throughout — pairs badly with the warmth intergenerational practice needs.
  Consider as a single accent, not as a system colour.

### 4. Sasha Waltz & Guests

![Sasha Waltz & Guests homepage](../../raw/assets/inspiration/sasha-waltz.png)

**URL:** <https://www.sashawaltz.de> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer ·
stark-typography · German-design · low-density-hero · collective-naming

**Why it's here.** 30+ year Berlin company with the deliberate "& Guests" framing — a
quietly elegant collective posture. Similar relational stance to Leon's Seppe-and-the-
collective tension.

**Observations.**

- Black hero, only the wordmark "Sasha Waltz & Guests" centered in light-gray sans-serif.
- Tiny lowercase replica of the wordmark top-left as logo. Top-right: small venue icon,
  search, "Menü" (hamburger pattern → off-canvas nav).
- Chevron at bottom signals scroll. No other content above the fold.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ *"& Guests" naming as collective device.* A quietly elegant phrasing that holds
  choreographer-and-collective without forcing one frame. Worth studying for Leon's own
  copy: how do we sit Seppe and the collective in a single phrase?
- ⚠️ *Hidden-nav (Menü)* lets the homepage breathe. Trade-off with Leon's
  [4-item visible primary nav](30-structure.md) — Leon's call is right (findability for
  first-timer participants), but the *visual restraint* underneath is the lesson.
- *Stark typographic discipline* = institutional gravitas, German workhorse-sans
  register. Carries the visitekaartje load strongly.

**What NOT to copy.**

- Empty-hero approach — Leon's audience mix (jury + participant + groep) has more
  variance than this site assumes; a black void asks too much of a first-timer
  participant ([VP register-pair](../strategy/40-value-proposition.md)).
- "Menü"-only nav fights findability — [Structure](30-structure.md) is right to make
  the 4 primary destinations visible at all times.

### 5. Compagnie Käfig (Mourad Merzouki)

![Compagnie Käfig homepage](../../raw/assets/inspiration/mourad-merzouki.png)

**URL:** <https://kafig.com> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer · hip-hop ·
30-year-legacy · language-toggle · choreographer-vs-collective

**Why it's here.** 30-year hip-hop dance company with community/intergenerational roots.
The site is more theatrical than the underlying community practice — a register-mismatch
worth studying as a *caution*.

**Observations.**

- Hero = full-bleed dark theatrical photo: dancers suspended on aerial poles around a
  classical column, dramatic lighting.
- Wordmark "käfig" in fine serif, center-top, with "Direction Mourad Merzouki" under.
- Horizontal nav: *Agenda · Compagnie · Spectacles · Mourad Merzouki · Récréer.com ·
  Contact · 30 ans de KÄFIG.*
- Top-right: clean "FR | EN" language toggle.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ *Bilingual toggle (FR | EN) at top-right* is the standard, clean implementation —
  Leon's EN/FR/NL needs slightly more weight (3 codes) but the pattern carries.
- ⚠️ *Choreographer as separate nav item* ("Mourad Merzouki" sits beside "Compagnie")
  addresses the choreographer-vs-collective tension head-on. Leon could similarly let
  "Seppe Baeyens" be findable as a person *and* keep the org page distinct, without one
  swallowing the other.
- *Anniversary as nav item* (*"30 ans de KÄFIG"*) — temporary-content categories handled
  at primary-nav level. Useful for Leon if specific projects (an edition, an anniversary
  programme) deserve top-level prominence for a time-bound window.

**What NOT to copy.**

- The hero is highly theatrical-spectacle; Merzouki's actual community/intergenerational
  work isn't visible from the homepage. *Register mismatch:* Leon must NOT open with a
  spectacle photo when the practice is participatory
  ([VP / register-pair](../strategy/40-value-proposition.md)).
- 7-item horizontal nav exceeds Leon's [4-item rule](30-structure.md).

### 6. Hofesh Shechter Company

![Hofesh Shechter Company homepage with player error](../../raw/assets/inspiration/hofesh-shechter.png)

**URL:** <https://hofesh.co.uk> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer · UK ·
multi-entity-brand · video-as-hero-(fragile)

**Why it's here.** Major UK choreographer running a multi-entity company (main company,
Shechter II, Apprenticeship, Junior, education arm). Useful for *one-brand-many-entry-
points* patterns and — accidentally — for what happens when the hero video fails to
load.

**Observations.**

- The capture **caught a "Player error" overlay** — the hero video failed at load. The
  fallback view shows: small wordmark "hofesh" top-left; standard utility nav (search,
  menu); a fallback grey field where the video would be; two columns of plain-text
  announcements below ("Hofesh appointed Guest Artistic Director...", "Workshop in
  Redruth..."); small image of a dance ensemble bottom-right.
- Editorial blocks are the actual content carrier when the video is gone.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ The fallback view is *accidentally* a maintainability stress-test. The page survives
  without its hero: editorial copy, dated announcements, image-as-supporting-illustration.
  Leon's home should similarly **survive without its hero image** (network failure, slow
  connection, JS off, image rights revoked). A useful gate for
  [maintainability](20-scope.md).
- ⚠️ *Multi-entity brand under one site* (main + junior + apprentice + education) — a
  pattern relevant if Leon ever needs to surface *Mariage* / *Birds* / *INVITED* as
  distinct but kin entities, sharing chrome.

**What NOT to copy.**

- Reliance on autoplay video as the hero load — we literally just witnessed it fail. Do
  not make video a critical-path element of Leon's first-impression frame.
- The capture is honestly a *degraded* reference. Re-visit when their player is fixed if
  the entry survives the north-star cut.

### 7. Bill T. Jones / Arnie Zane Company (NYLA)

![Bill T. Jones / Arnie Zane Company page on New York Live Arts](../../raw/assets/inspiration/bill-t-jones.png)

**URL:** <https://newyorklivearts.org/btj-az-company/> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 ·
**Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer · resident-company-on-venue-site · strong-brand-system ·
single-accent-color

**Why it's here.** Bill T. Jones / Arnie Zane Company merged with Dance Theater Workshop
in 2011 to form New York Live Arts. They now live as a resident company on the venue's
site — an interesting precedent for how a participatory/social-practice dance company
can be presented within a larger institutional frame.

**Observations.**

- Top: ticker-style overlapping headline — green "NEW YORK LIVE ARTS" with white "BILL T.
  JONES" laid across it in big sans-serif caps. Bold typographic device.
- Primary nav: *Season & Tickets · Bill T. Jones / Arnie Zane · About · Support.*
- Page body, two columns: production photo (company in red costumes, stage); right
  column = green-highlighted text-link list (*ABOUT BILL T. JONES / ARNIE ZANE COMPANY ·
  OUR WORK · ABOUT BILL T. JONES · ABOUT ARNIE ZANE · PERFORMERS & COLLABORATORS · PRESS*).
- Bright green is the single accent color carrying the brand throughout.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ *Resident-company-on-parent-site pattern.* When a dance practice lives inside a
  larger entity, the page becomes "their corner of the building". A loose analog for
  Leon: *Mariage* / *Birds* / *INVITED* each as a "room" within leon.dance — internal
  coherence per project, shared chrome across the site
  ([Structure](30-structure.md) *one canonical page per project* rule).
- ⚠️ *Commitment to a single accent color* as identity carrier — clarity and
  recognisability without elaborate palette work. Leon likely wants warmth not loud-
  green, but the *discipline of one accent* is worth importing.
- *Right-column text-link list* — calm wayfinding pattern for a content-heavy section
  page. Useful for project-detail pages where multiple sub-views (about, work,
  collaborators, press) need to coexist.

**What NOT to copy.**

- The BTJ-AZ identity is *subordinated* to NYLA's design system — Leon has no parent
  venue, so the brand must be the dominant frame.
- Ticker-overlay headline is visually loud and likely poor on mobile — not a pattern
  to import for Leon's first-impression frame.

### 8. Studios Kabako (Faustin Linyekula)

![Studios Kabako homepage — raw text-first site](../../raw/assets/inspiration/faustin-linyekula.png)

**URL:** <https://kabako.org> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:** 🎭 dance-peer · DRC ·
raw-aesthetic · text-first · radical-maintainability

**Why it's here.** Faustin Linyekula's Studios Kabako, based in Kisangani (DRC), operates
with radically different constraints. Including this pulls the inspiration set away from
European-production polish, and reminds us — bluntly — that *good design* in this sector
can be local, raw, and document-first. A useful corrective to Awwwards-bait drift.

**Observations.**

- Effectively no visual design system beyond HTML defaults + minimal CSS. White
  background, system-font black body text.
- Vertical left text-nav in tiny sans-serif: *accueil · studio · création · diffusion ·
  résidences · médiathèque · partenaires · presse · contacts.*
- Tiny site-mark top: *studios kabako / Faustin Linyekula / Direction Artistique.*
- Body content = a French manifesto-paragraph dominating the visible area. One small
  documentary photograph bottom-left. That is the entire fold.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ ***Maintainability gate, embodied.*** A site clearly kept alive on a tiny budget
  for ~20 years. No CMS animations, no hero videos, nothing that needs designer-on-call.
  Passes Leon's [maintainability gate](20-scope.md) in the most extreme way — a useful
  reality-check on every "wouldn't it be cool if..." moment ahead.
- ⚠️ *Text-first content model.* When the practice is the message, ornament gets in the
  way. Reminds Leon that **strong writing is half of "great UI" in this sector** —
  no surface treatment compensates for thin copy.
- *French content register* shows what a dance-organisation manifesto reads like on the
  page — useful for tonal calibration of Leon's "Over Leon" copy.

**What NOT to copy.**

- The visual abdication. Leon's
  [G1 Kunstendecreet jury rubric](../strategy/10-organisation-goals.md) needs legitimacy
  signaling that this site does not give. Don't import the surface; *do* import the
  editorial discipline and the maintainability ethic.

### 9. Serpentine (Galleries)

![Serpentine homepage](../../raw/assets/inspiration/serpentine.png)

**URL:** <https://www.serpentinegalleries.org> · **Captured** 2026-05-26 · **Tags:**
🏛 institution · UK · editorial-polish · heavy-typography · 4-item-nav

**Why it's here.** London contemporary art gallery known for confident editorial output
and gallery-grade typographic identity. A "museum that doesn't apologise" reference —
institutional gravitas without coldness.

**Observations.**

- Black header strip with "SERPENTINE" wordmark in heavy white grotesk caps, left-
  aligned. Top utility strip (small white text) carries a pre-booking notice.
- Primary nav, 4 items: *WHAT'S ON · PLAN YOUR VISIT · SHOP · MENU.*
- Hero = curatorial photograph (David Hockney installation, gallery interior, natural
  lighting). Overlay text: *"DAVID HOCKNEY"* (large white sans), *"NOW OPEN"* below.
- Below the fold: *WHAT'S ON* section header with subtext invitation.

**Transferable to Leon.**

- ⚠️ *4-item primary nav* exactly matches Leon's [Structure](30-structure.md) rule.
  Their categorisation (calendar / transactional / commercial / overflow) differs from
  Leon's, but the **discipline of four** is identical.
- ⚠️ *Heavy serif/grotesk wordmark in caps.* Confidence comes from typographic *weight*,
  not elaboration — useful counter-pressure if Leon's wordmark is felt as "too quiet".
  Gravity is a font-weight decision, not a colour-palette decision.
- *Notice strip pattern* (top of viewport) for time-sensitive announcements — maps
  onto Leon's [Agenda dual-job](30-structure.md): a slot for "Atelier Leon open dit

## North-star deep dives

Of the 18 references, **8 rise above the rest as direct inputs for Leon's Surface
decisions.** They aren't the "best designed" sites — they're the ones whose specific
patterns Leon should actively try to import or react to. Each carries a pointed lesson;
together they triangulate the visual direction.

### The 8 north-stars

1. **[Boris Charmatz / [terrain]](#2-boris-charmatz--terrain)** — *Photo discipline.* THE
   primary reference. Non-professional bodies, real space, mixed ages, photographed with
   documentary honesty. **Every photo Leon considers for the home should be screened
   against this hero.** The wordmark stays calm because the photo does the heavy lifting.
   ⚠️ Single-language is the only major caveat.
2. **[Dance Exchange (Liz Lerman)](#1-dance-exchange-liz-lerman-legacy)** —
   *Register-pair in one frame.* Mission statement laid over a real intergenerational-
   community photo: institutional articulation + participatory warmth without a toggle.
   The maintainable proof that visitekaartje + invitation can coexist in a single fold.
3. **[Pro Helvetia](#12-pro-helvetia)** — *Multilingual + institutional-playful palette.*
   The CH 4-language benchmark, with multilingualism *in the wordmark*. Pill nav + bright
   accent + clear primary CTA — exactly the rare visual register Leon needs to resolve
   funder-credibility with participant-invitation at the chrome level.
4. **[KW Institute Berlin](#10-kw-institute-for-contemporary-art-berlin)** —
   *Plain-language as a first-class option.* The single most directly transferable
   inclusivity move. Adding *eenvoudige NL* alongside NL/FR/EN would do real
   participatory work — pending an honest editorial-capacity check.
5. **[Walker Art Center](#11-walker-art-center)** — *Single hero image + name + date
   range.* The cleanest project-edition page pattern in the set, with a warm-neutral
   background that gives photography air. Direct template for Leon's *Mariage* /
   *Birds* / *INVITED* edition pages.
6. **[Festival d'Avignon (80e)](#17-festival-davignon-80e)** — *Countdown + edition-
   number + single bold color.* "J-39" as live anchor, "80e Festival" as branding hook —
   Leon's project editions could carry similar mechanics (*Mariage 2025 (4e editie),
   première J-X*).
7. **[ImPulsTanz Vienna](#18-impulstanz-vienna-international-dance-festival)** —
   *Hand-drawn mark + intergenerational subject + magazine-cover composition.* The
   warm-half of the register-pair done at festival scale; subject choice (elderly
   woman) IS the position. The closest single reference for Leon's evenwaardig posture.
8. **[Are.na](#14-arena)** — *Text-first discipline + maintainability ethic.* Counter-
   pressure against ornament drift. When the writing is good, surface treatment becomes
   optional — relevant for the *Over Leon* register and every editorial dispatch Kristin
   will write.

### Themes across the north-stars

Cross-cutting patterns to carry into Surface decisions:

- ⚠️ ***Photo selection IS positioning.*** Charmatz, ImPulsTanz, Lerman, Walker — all
  four spend their hero on a single photograph whose *subject* does the work. Choice of
  body, age, place, light = the brand decision. This is the most important single
  Surface lesson, and it's a *content-editorial* commitment more than a visual-design
  one. Risk = Leon's [image map](90-image-map.md) is currently weighted toward
  *Mariage*/gallery skew; cross-check it against this lens.
- ⚠️ ***Multilingual surface as identity, not chrome.*** Pro Helvetia bakes Italian into
  the wordmark; KW puts *Einfache Sprache* in the language switcher; Avignon's *EN*
  toggle is clean and quiet. None treat multilingualism as a feature-flag — it's
  structural. Leon's EN/FR/NL spec ([Scope](20-scope.md)) should be designed accordingly.
- ⚠️ ***Calm wordmark vs. hot content.*** Charmatz (serif calm + documentary photo),
  Walker (center wordmark + colour photo), ImPulsTanz (small caption + bold portrait) all
  let the wordmark stay quiet. Heat lives in the content. A useful guard against
  over-designing Leon's mark.
- ⚠️ ***Time-anchored content keeps the site alive.*** Olafur Eliasson's *"13 days ago"*,
  Festival d'Avignon's *"J-39"*, Walker's *"Mar 28 – Aug 30, 2026"* — visible timestamps
  signal **living site, not catalogue**. Leon's home should never read as undated.
- ⚠️ ***Maintainability is a Surface concern, not just a Scope concern.*** Are.na,
  Kabako, Forsythe — three references whose discipline is *not adding visual complexity*.
  Maintainability isn't only about code; it's about not requiring designer-on-call to
  keep the site healthy ([Scope maintainability gate](20-scope.md)).

### Anti-patterns to actively avoid

Aggregating the *what NOT to copy* across the set, with the strongest cautions:

- *Autoplay video as hero load* — Hofesh just demonstrated the fragility.
- *Empty / void hero* — Sasha Waltz, Forsythe; too sparse for Leon's audience.
- *Dark museum chrome* — Serpentine, Akram Khan, KW; cool/exclusive register reads
  against invitation.
- *Hidden hamburger as primary nav* — Sasha Waltz, ImPulsTanz, Olafur Eliasson;
  conflicts with [Structure](30-structure.md) 4-item visible rule.
- *Transactional Tickets pill* — Walker; Leon explicitly is **not** a ticketing site
  ([Won't-Have list](20-scope.md)).
- *Maximalist single-color hero* — Avignon, Kafig; festival-scale, not daily-use scale.

### 🌬 Breathing · slow · cinematic

Sites where motion has phrasing — watch the *timing*, the *pauses*, the *rest beats*
between moves.

- [Mubi](https://mubi.com) — film stills crossfade with cinematic deliberation;
  transitions have weight.
- [A24](https://a24films.com) — film grid with elegant hover-reveals; titles emerge
  like opening credits.
- [Ableton — Learning Music](https://learningmusic.ableton.com) — scroll-driven
  musical phrases; the page IS a composition. Closest single demo of *site-as-phrasing*.
- [Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com) — case studies swim into view; pages
  choreographed as a single slow gesture.
- [Apple — AirPods Max](https://www.apple.com/airpods-max/) — paragon orchestrated-
  scroll: each section a phrase, fades and reveals timed like film cuts. (Marketing
  context, but the choreography is the lesson.)
- [ICA London](https://www.ica.art) — measured editorial pacing; image-reveals
  considered, not auto.
- [Studio Dumbar](https://studiodumbar.com) — motion-identity case studies (Dutch
  Police, Bayerische Staatsoper, ANWB) — see how brand systems unfold in motion at
  cultural scale.
- [Wayne McGregor](https://waynemcgregor.com) — choreographer site; identity calibrated
  to choreographic process; rare dance-specific case in this register.

### 👆 Cursor-as-choreographer · scroll-as-phrasing

Sites where the user's motion through the page IS the dance — hover, drag, and scroll
feel embodied. Watch your own cursor as you move.

- [Locomotive](https://locomotive.ca) — Canadian studio; authors of `locomotive-scroll`,
  their site is the canonical demo. Buttery weighted scroll with gravity.
- [Hello Monday](https://hellomonday.com) — Danish/NY motion-design studio;
  sophisticated cursor-reactive identity.
- [Anti](https://anti.as) — Norwegian studio; cursor-driven UI revelations and
  transitions, restrained-but-alive.
- [Active Theory](https://activetheory.net) — LA interactive studio; cinematic case
  studies with cursor-led reveals.
- [Rauno Freiberg](https://rauno.me) — designer portfolio; micro-motion done right —
  every cursor interaction feels deliberate, never showy.
- [Linear](https://linear.app) — extremely refined micro-motion + scroll-revealed
  product story; pristine but warm (closest "athletic-precise" the warmer set
  tolerates).
- [Klim Type Foundry](https://klim.co.nz) — type specimen pages where scroll
  choreographs letterform reveal; type IS the dancer.
- [Studio Feixen](https://www.studiofeixen.ch) — Swiss studio; cursor-interactive,
  playful-but-considered.
- [Bruno Simon — portfolio](https://bruno-simon.com) — extreme case (drive a 3D car
  around a portfolio); not for Leon literally, but the cleanest *agency-of-user-motion*
  illustration in the field.

### Anti-patterns specific to motion (guardrail)

- *Scroll-jacking that fights the user* — orchestrated scroll becomes hostile when it
  refuses normal browser behaviour. Apple Pro Display XDR page = the classic warning.
- *Cursor effects without semantic purpose* — magnetic buttons, custom cursors, etc.
  when they don't *say* anything. Decoration disguised as craft.
- *Motion-everywhere syndrome* — when every element has a transition, nothing has
  weight. Stillness is rhythm too.
